Transcript (English translation)

Translated from the Turkish original; the Turkish text prevails.

As you said, we have filed our action for annulment at the 6th Chamber of the Council of State (Danıştay) against the Presidency's urgent expropriation decision.

In the case at issue, in order to remove you, through the measure taken, from your land, from the fields you sow and reap, from your homes, from your [unclear], and for a short-lived, unsustainable open-pit mining operation, for a coal of worthless nature and low quality that will come out of the ground, out from underground, [unclear] want to drive you out of this land where you were born and raised.

Here, the groundwork is being prepared for bringing back in the future the thermal power plant project that was previously cancelled. Look, the people of this area waged their struggle against the thermal power plant at that time too, came out of that struggle victorious, and protected their land and their environment.

Again, we are here today for the same struggle, to protect your rights, the rights of your land, of the plain where you were born and raised, of the water you drink, of the animals roaming the mountains and of the birds flying in the sky. So no one will give up their rights. We will not let a mine be opened here. Thank you.
