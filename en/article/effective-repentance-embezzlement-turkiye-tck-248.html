Effective Repentance in Embezzlement under Turkish Law: Penal Code Art. 248, Reductions, Full Compensation

Short answer: Under Turkish law, effective repentance in embezzlement means a reduction of the penalty where the embezzled property is returned in kind or the loss caused is fully compensated. The reduction is fixed by statute according to the stage at which the return or compensation takes place: two thirds of the penalty before the investigation begins, one half for voluntary return or compensation before the prosecution begins, and one third before judgment. According to the Court of Cassation, partial payment and collection through enforcement proceedings do not qualify for this reduction; the amount embezzled is, as a rule, determined by its value at the time of the offence, without interest. In embezzlement by use, the subject of the offence is not the money itself but its yield; effective repentance does not apply unless the yield is paid.

This article explains Turkish law. It is an English translation of the Turkish original; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

In brief

Effective repentance in embezzlement under Turkish Penal Code art. 248: reductions of two thirds before the investigation, one half before the prosecution and one third before judgment where the property is returned or the loss fully compensated; partial payment, collection through enforcement, interest, yield in embezzlement by use, payment after reversal and the link with HAGB, with Court of Cassation decisions.

Effective repentance (Turkish Penal Code art. 248) in the offence of embezzlement by a public official (Turkish Penal Code art. 247): return of the property in kind or full compensation of the loss, fixed reduction rates by stage (investigation, prosecution, judgment), interest and yield, the relationship with embezzlement by use and fraudulent embezzlement, proof of payment and the link with deferral of the announcement of the verdict (Code of Criminal Procedure art. 231); with decisions of the Criminal General Assembly and the 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation.

What Is Effective Repentance in Embezzlement? (Penal Code arts. 247–248)

Under Turkish law, embezzlement (zimmet) is the offence of a public official who appropriates, for himself or for another, property whose possession was transferred to him by reason of his duty or which he is obliged to protect and supervise. The Code regulates the offence in three forms: simple embezzlement (art. 247/1), qualified embezzlement committed through fraudulent conduct aimed at concealing the embezzlement (art. 247/2), and embezzlement by use, committed with the intention of returning the property after using it temporarily (art. 247/3).

Statutory Provision — Turkish Penal Code (Law No. 5237) art. 247 (Embezzlement) (unofficial translation)

“(1) A public official who appropriates, for himself or for another, property whose possession was transferred to him by reason of his duty or which he is obliged to protect and supervise shall be punished with imprisonment from five to twelve years.

(2) Where the offence is committed through fraudulent conduct aimed at ensuring that the embezzlement is not discovered, the penalty to be imposed shall be increased by one half.

(3) Where the offence of embezzlement is committed with the intention of returning the property after using it for a temporary period, the penalty to be imposed may be reduced by up to one half.”

Effective repentance is an institution that rewards the offender who makes good the loss after the offence has been completed with a reduction of the penalty. The text of the article does not distinguish between simple, qualified and use embezzlement; the decisions below show it being applied in all three forms. The current text in the Official Legislation Information System carries no amendment note for arts. 247 and 248.

Statutory Provision — Turkish Penal Code (Law No. 5237) art. 248 (Effective repentance) (unofficial translation)

“(1) Where, before the investigation begins, the embezzled property is returned in kind or the loss incurred is fully compensated, two thirds of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced.

(2) Where, before the prosecution begins, the embezzled property is voluntarily returned in kind or the loss incurred is fully compensated, one half of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced. Where effective repentance takes place before judgment, one third of the penalty to be imposed shall be reduced.”

Conditions: Return in Kind or Full Compensation

Full restitution. The Code recognises two routes: return of the embezzled property in kind, or full compensation of the loss incurred. The article provides no separate reduction rate for partial return or compensation. The 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation has treated the application of effective repentance to a defendant who did not compensate the whole embezzled amount as a ground for reversal (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2022/7409, K. 2025/13603).

Voluntariness and enforcement. The words “voluntarily” appear in the text only in art. 248/2. The Court of Cassation, however, requires the return or compensation to be voluntary for effective repentance and does not treat money collected from the defendant through enforcement proceedings as a voluntary return. In the same decision, it required a payment made directly into the account of the participating institution, without the pressure of enforcement proceedings, to be assessed after verifying the authenticity of the bank receipt (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/2659, K. 2024/11174). In another case, applying effective repentance was held unlawful where the institution’s loss had been “made good through deductions made by way of enforcement from the employees’ salaries” and the defendant had made no voluntary payment; in that case the Chamber’s main ground for reversal was that the act constituted qualified fraud, and its assessment of effective repentance was made “on the assumption of the finding” (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2023/8747, K. 2026/8434).

Several defendants. The 5th Criminal Chamber accepts that a payment made by one defendant also benefits the other defendants who do not object to that payment (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/4010, K. 2025/7374). No general rule on payments made by persons who are not defendants emerges from the decisions discussed in this article.

Reductions by Stage: Two Thirds, One Half, One Third

The reduction rates are fixed by statute: the article says the penalty “shall be reduced”, not that it “may be reduced” or reduced “up to” a certain fraction. The court’s discretion lies not in the rate but in determining the stage at which the return or compensation took place.

  • Before the investigation begins, return in kind or full compensation: two thirds of the penalty is reduced (art. 248/1).
  • Before the prosecution begins, voluntary return in kind or full compensation: one half of the penalty is reduced (art. 248/2, first sentence).
  • Effective repentance before judgment: one third of the penalty is reduced (art. 248/2, second sentence).

When does the investigation begin? According to the Criminal General Assembly of the Court of Cassation, the investigation stage begins when the public prosecutor learns of the suspicion of an offence. The General Assembly upheld the application of art. 248/1 to a convicted person who returned the embezzled amount while an internal administrative inquiry was under way, before the matter was reported to the prosecutor’s office; it also stated that the effective repentance provision for bribery cannot be applied to embezzlement by analogy (Criminal General Assembly, E. 2006/165, K. 2006/213). The objection of the Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office of the Court of Cassation, which argued that the investigation begins with the institution’s first intervention in the offence, was dismissed. The decision dates from 2006; a 2024 decision of the 5th Criminal Chamber likewise states that art. 248/1 must be applied to a payment made before the investigation began (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/15860, K. 2024/10964).

Under the definition in the Code of Criminal Procedure quoted by the General Assembly, the investigation is the stage from the moment the suspicion of an offence is learned until the indictment is accepted. Accordingly, a voluntary return made after the prosecutor has learned of the matter but before the indictment is accepted leads to a one-half reduction; a return made after the indictment is accepted but before judgment leads to a one-third reduction. Applying the wrong paragraph is a ground for reversal: the Chamber reversed a judgment for this reason where a one-third reduction instead of two thirds had been applied to a defendant who paid before the investigation began (K. 2024/10964).

Payment after a reversal. The “before judgment” limit is not confined to the first judgment. In a case where the loss was fully paid after the Court of Cassation’s reversal judgment but before the new judgment, the Chamber described the refusal to apply the last sentence of art. 248/2 as resting on “reasoning without legal basis” (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2020/2405, K. 2023/2719).

Scope and Determination of the Loss: Interest, Value at the Time of the Offence, Expert Reports

Interest and indexation. According to the 5th Criminal Chamber, in determining the value of the embezzled property, default interest or late-payment surcharge, the loss suffered by the administration, lost income and any additional benefit the defendant obtained are not taken into account; the value at the time the offence was committed is decisive. In that case the Chamber treated a payment covering the embezzled amount remaining after deduction of interest as effective repentance before the investigation (K. 2024/10964). Along the same line, it was accepted that art. 248/2 must be applied to a defendant who returned the principal, being the actual embezzled amount, before judgment (K. 2025/7374). These decisions show only the amount required for effective repentance; they do not concern whether the institution may claim interest and similar items by other means.

Cases where the amount is uncertain. In most cases the embezzled amount is established by an inspector’s review and an expert report. The Chamber treated as a ground for reversal the refusal to apply effective repentance to a defendant who had shown an intention to pay by handing over the items concerned, without first asking whether he would pay the price difference established by the expert report and, where necessary, giving him a reasonable time (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2018/6932, K. 2022/3092). Where a defendant who paid within the time set by the court submits the bank receipt late, the authenticity of the receipt must be examined (K. 2024/11174).

A different limit applies at the investigation stage: in a decision rendered on an application for reversal in the interest of the law, the Chamber concluded that “not giving the suspect a reasonable time to make good the loss is not among the grounds for returning the indictment” (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2023/10298, K. 2023/11932). An opportunity to pay at the investigation stage may therefore not arise of its own accord.

Relationship with Embezzlement by Use and Fraudulent Embezzlement

Embezzlement by use (art. 247/3). Where property is appropriated with the intention of returning it after temporary use, the penalty may be reduced “by up to one half”; unlike the rates in art. 248, this rate is discretionary. According to the Court of Cassation, in embezzlement by use the subject of the offence is the yield obtained from money used temporarily and returned; the amount of yield must be calculated by an expert, and effective repentance must not be applied unless the yield is paid (5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2022/4230, K. 2025/1686). The fact that the money itself was paid in, even late, is therefore not regarded as sufficient on its own. The decision indicates that, where the yield is also paid, art. 248 is to be assessed in addition to the reduction under art. 247/3.

Fraudulent (qualified) embezzlement (art. 247/2). In embezzlement committed through fraudulent conduct, the penalty is increased by one half; effective repentance applies to this form as well. The Chamber stated that art. 248/2 must be applied to a defendant convicted of qualified embezzlement who returned the principal before judgment (K. 2025/7374). Whether the offence is simple or qualified determines the penalty to which the reduction is applied: in a case where the method of appropriating the money amounted to crude deception and came to light on simple examination, the Chamber classified the act as simple embezzlement and, in the same decision, required the one-third reduction to be applied to a payment made after the reversal (K. 2023/2719).

Procedure and Deferral of the Announcement of the Verdict (HAGB)

Chamber and reasoning. All of the 2022–2026 embezzlement decisions discussed in this article were rendered by the 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation. Because the rates are fixed, the court’s main task is to establish the date and amount of the return or compensation and whether it was voluntary, and to discuss these in its reasoning; the Chamber stresses that the conditions of effective repentance must be “discussed in the judgment” (K. 2024/11174).

HAGB. Deferral of the announcement of the verdict requires that the penalty imposed be two years or less and that the loss suffered by the victim or the public be fully made good; where the loss cannot be made good immediately, deferral may also be ordered on condition that it is paid in monthly instalments during the supervision period. Since the basic penalty for embezzlement is five to twelve years, the two-year limit can be reached only through reductions. As a hypothetical calculation: if the basic penalty is set at five years and reduced by two thirds for effective repentance before the investigation, the penalty becomes one year and eight months; other increases and reductions change this result. The option to pay in instalments relates only to the HAGB condition; according to the text, the reduction under art. 248 depends on a return or compensation completed before judgment at the latest.

Statutory Provision — Code of Criminal Procedure (Law No. 5271) art. 231/5, 6-c, 9 (Deferral of the announcement of the verdict) (unofficial translation)

“(5) … Where the penalty imposed at the end of the trial for the offence attributed to the defendant is imprisonment of two years or less or a judicial fine, the court may decide to defer the announcement of the verdict.

… (6) … For a decision deferring the announcement of the verdict to be rendered; … c) the loss suffered by the victim or the public through the commission of the offence must be fully made good by return in kind, restoration to the situation before the offence or compensation.

… (9) … Where the defendant cannot immediately fulfil the condition set out in subparagraph (c) of the sixth paragraph, a decision deferring the announcement of the verdict may also be rendered on condition that the defendant fully makes good the loss caused to the victim or the public by paying it in monthly instalments during the supervision period.”

Court of Cassation Decisions

The quotations in the boxes are taken only from the Court of Cassation’s own reasoning; the reasoning of first-instance courts, the opinions of the Chief Public Prosecutor’s Office and dissenting opinions are not quoted.

Start of the investigation; payment during an administrative inquiry.

Court of Cassation Criminal General Assembly Decision
Criminal General Assembly, E. 2006/165, K. 2006/213, 17.10.2006 (proceedings to apply the more favourable law; reasoning of the General Assembly; the objection was unanimously dismissed)

“Since the ‘investigation stage’ therefore begins when the suspicion of an offence is learned by the public prosecutor, in cases where a reduction is to be made for ‘effective repentance’ under article 248 of Turkish Penal Code No. 5237, the first paragraph must be applied where the payment is made before the matter is learned by the public prosecutor’s office and investigative steps begin, and the second paragraph in other cases. … As can be seen, the embezzled amount was returned before any report of the embezzlement offence was made to the public prosecutor’s office.”

Value at the time of the offence, excluding interest; payment before the investigation.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/15860, K. 2024/10964, 05.11.2024 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning)

“in determining the value of the embezzled property, its value at the time the offence was committed must be taken into account, without regard to default interest or late-payment surcharge, the loss suffered by the administration, lost income or any additional benefit the defendant obtained from the embezzled item … accordingly, imposing an excessive penalty by applying a one-third reduction under article 248/2 of Turkish Penal Code No. 5237 instead of two thirds under article 248/1 of the same Code, without taking into account that effective repentance took place before the investigation”

Return of the principal and payment by a co-defendant.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/4010, K. 2025/7374, 24.06.2025 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning; qualified embezzlement)

“failing to take into account, bearing in mind that the other defendants who do not object to a payment made by one of the defendants also benefit from the effective repentance provisions on account of that payment, that article 248/2 of Law No. 5237 must also be applied to the defendant who showed effective repentance by returning the principal, being the actual embezzled amount, to the cooperative before judgment”

Partial compensation is not enough.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2022/7409, K. 2025/13603, 25.11.2025 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning)

“although the application of the effective repentance provisions requires that the embezzled property be returned in kind or the loss incurred be fully compensated, applying the effective repentance provision in article 248 of Law No. 5237 to a defendant who did not compensate the whole embezzled amount, even though its legal conditions were not met”

Collection through enforcement; payment directly into the institution’s account and examination of the receipt.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2021/2659, K. 2024/11174, 19.11.2024 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning)

“since, where the embezzled money is collected from the defendant through enforcement proceedings, there is no voluntary return, the defendant cannot benefit from the effective repentance provisions; in the specific case, however, given that the payment was made directly into the account of the participating institution without the pressure of enforcement proceedings, … failing to take into account that it should have been discussed in the judgment whether the conditions for applying the effective repentance provisions in article 248/2 of Turkish Penal Code No. 5237 were met, after examining the authenticity of the bank receipt dated 17.03.2016 showing that the institution’s loss had been made good”

Payment after reversal, before judgment.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2020/2405, K. 2023/2719, 14.03.2023 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning)

“although the court also accepted that the defendant showed effective repentance at the prosecution stage by paying the whole embezzled amount before judgment, and a one-third reduction should have been applied under the last sentence of the second paragraph of article 248 of Law No. 5237, not applying the last sentence of the second paragraph of article 248 of Law No. 5237 on the reasoning, without legal basis, that the effective repentance provision cannot be applied because the loss suffered by the public was made good after the Court of Cassation’s reversal judgment”

Yield in embezzlement by use.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2022/4230, K. 2025/1686, 06.02.2025 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning; applying effective repentance without payment of the yield was treated as a ground for reversal “on the assumption of the finding”)

“as regards the defendant’s acts that may be assessed as embezzlement by use, since the subject of the offence is the yield obtained from money used temporarily and returned, having the ‘amount of yield’ calculated by an expert and determining the defendant’s legal position according to the result, upon establishing the total embezzled amount … as regards the defendant’s acts accepted as embezzlement by use, applying the effective repentance provisions under article 248 of Law No. 5237 although the defendant did not pay the yield of the money used temporarily”

Giving time to a defendant who has shown an intention to pay.

Court of Cassation Decision
5th Criminal Chamber, E. 2018/6932, K. 2022/3092, 15.03.2022 (the Chamber’s reversal reasoning)

“although the defendant’s legal position should have been determined after asking the defendant, who had shown an intention to pay, whether he would pay the amount of the loss relating to the price difference between the items handed over and the items received, as established by the expert report dated 11/06/2015, and, where necessary, after giving him a reasonable time for this, not applying the effective repentance provisions to the defendant without taking his statement on this matter”

Practical Points

  • Timing: The date of payment determines the reduction rate. Payments made before the prosecutor learns of the matter, before the indictment is accepted and before judgment have different consequences; payment can also be made after a reversal, before the new judgment.
  • Completeness: Partial payment does not secure the reduction under art. 248. If the embezzled amount is disputed, clarifying the amount first and then requesting time to pay may come into play.
  • Amount: Compare the embezzled amount with the inspector’s and expert reports. According to the Court of Cassation, interest, late-payment surcharges and lost income are not added to the value of the embezzlement; in embezzlement by use, however, the yield must be calculated and paid.
  • Method of payment: Making the payment without the pressure of enforcement, directly into the account of the institution that suffered the loss, and filing the bank receipt without delay, makes it easier to prove voluntariness and the date.
  • Institutional records (for institutions’ lawyers): The collection letter should state clearly the date, the amount and the method of collection (voluntary payment, salary deduction, enforcement); the Court of Cassation bases its assessment of voluntariness on these records.
  • Several defendants: Payment of a joint embezzled amount by one defendant is also assessed in favour of the other defendants who do not object to the payment.
  • HAGB: The two-year limit and the condition of fully making good the loss are examined separately from the effective repentance reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How large is the effective repentance reduction in embezzlement?

If the embezzled property is returned in kind or the loss fully compensated before the investigation begins, two thirds of the penalty is reduced; if this happens voluntarily before the prosecution begins, one half; and if it happens before judgment, one third (Turkish Penal Code art. 248). The rates are fixed; the court determines the stage at which the return or compensation took place and applies the corresponding rate.

Does paying part of the loss reduce the penalty?

Art. 248 requires the return of the property in kind or full compensation of the loss and provides no separate rate for partial payment. The 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation has treated the application of effective repentance to a defendant who did not compensate the whole embezzled amount as a ground for reversal.

Must interest also be paid for effective repentance?

According to the 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation, default interest or late-payment surcharges, the loss suffered by the administration and lost income are not taken into account in determining the value of the embezzled property; the value at the time of the offence is decisive. In embezzlement by use, however, the subject of the offence is the yield of the money; effective repentance does not apply unless the yield is paid.

Does effective repentance apply if the institution recovers the loss by salary deductions or enforcement?

The Court of Cassation does not treat money collected through enforcement as a voluntary return, and in one case it held the application of effective repentance unlawful where the loss had been made good through salary deductions made by way of enforcement. By contrast, a payment made directly into the institution’s account without the pressure of enforcement must be assessed after verifying the authenticity of the bank receipt.

Does a payment made after a reversal by the Court of Cassation reduce the penalty?

According to a decision of the 5th Criminal Chamber of the Court of Cassation, it does if made before the new judgment: in a case where the loss was fully paid after the reversal judgment but before judgment, it was held that a one-third reduction must be applied under the last sentence of art. 248/2.
