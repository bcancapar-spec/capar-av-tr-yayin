This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

Family law disputes have a strong human and emotional side and lasting consequences. In divorce, custody, maintenance and matrimonial property matters, the office follows an approach that takes into account the interests of the client and of the children.

Advice and litigation services are provided in uncontested and contested divorce, maintenance and custody, and the liquidation of the matrimonial property regime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an uncontested divorce take?

If the spouses agree on every issue, the process can be completed relatively quickly by preparing a protocol and attending a hearing.

How is custody decided?

In custody matters the best interests of the child are paramount; age, attachment and the circumstances of the parents are assessed together.

How is property divided?

Acquired property is liquidated according to the matrimonial property regime in force during the marriage; contribution and value-increase shares may be claimed separately.
