This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

A work accident or an occupational disease can have serious consequences for a worker's physical and mental integrity. Compensation for the losses suffered by the worker and their relatives depends on establishing fault and the causal link correctly.

In claims for work accident compensation, permanent incapacity, loss of financial support and non-pecuniary damages, the office aims to prevent any loss of the client's rights, taking expert and actuarial reports into account.

Labor & Social Security LawIs a Heart Attack at Work a Work Accident in Türkiye? Law No. 5510 Art. 13 and Court of Cassation Decisions19 November 2026Under Turkish law, a heart attack suffered by an insured while at the workplace is, as a rule, treated as a work accident under Article 13 of Law No. 5510 in decisions of the Court of Cassation’s 10th Civil Chamber; a heart attack in an employer’s shuttle was also accepted. A heart attack suffered by an insured sent on duty, at a place unrelated to the work, was not. The article covers the conditions, the mandatory SGK application and decisions with dissents.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the limitation period for work accident compensation?

Compensation claims arising from a work accident are, as a rule, subject to a ten-year limitation period; as this may vary with the nature of the incident, it is important to confirm the period with a lawyer.

How is the amount of compensation determined?

Compensation is determined by an expert's calculation based on factors such as the degree of fault, the degree of disability, income and age. Every case is different.

What should I do first after a work accident?

It is essential to have the accident recorded and to preserve medical reports and witness details. Collecting evidence early strengthens the pursuit of your rights.
