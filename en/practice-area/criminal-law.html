This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

In criminal proceedings every stage concerns a person's liberty; effective defence from the very start of the investigation is therefore critical. The office carries out a careful procedural and substantive analysis as defence counsel for suspects and defendants and as counsel for victims and intervening parties.

In the course of criminal proceedings, the assessment of evidence, the identification of procedural irregularities and bringing favourable points to the fore can be decisive for the outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I call a lawyer if I am taken into custody?

Yes. A suspect has the right to the assistance of defence counsel; speaking with a lawyer before giving a statement is one of the most basic safeguards.

How long does a criminal case take?

The duration depends on the nature of the offence, the state of the evidence and the court's workload. An early and well-founded defence strategy has a positive effect on the process.

A case has been brought against me. What should I do?

Examining the indictment and the case file with a lawyer and setting up the defence strategy from the outset prevents a loss of rights.
