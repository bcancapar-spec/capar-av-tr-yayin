This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

Acts of the administration directly affect people's rights in many areas, such as appointments, penalties, licences and tenders. An action for annulment brought in time against an unlawful administrative act is the most effective means of protection.

The office provides advice and litigation services in disputes concerning disciplinary penalties, administrative fines, losses caused by administrative acts and actions, and public procurement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a disciplinary penalty challenged?

A disciplinary penalty may first be challenged by an administrative objection and then, in time, by an action for annulment before the administrative courts. The periods are short; the date of notification is decisive.

What is the time limit for bringing an action for annulment?

As a rule, an action for annulment against an administrative act is brought within sixty days of notification. Missing the period has consequences that are difficult to remedy.

I suffered a loss because of an administrative act. Can I claim compensation?

Losses caused by an unlawful administrative act or action may be claimed through a full remedy action.
