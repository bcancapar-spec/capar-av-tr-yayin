This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

For most people real estate is their most valuable asset, and disputes over title deeds, leases and ownership require careful legal examination. The office protects the client's ownership rights in transactions and lawsuits concerning real estate.

Advice and litigation services are provided in the annulment and re-registration of title deeds, lease relationships, condominium ownership and expropriation proceedings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I evict my tenant?

Eviction depends on the contract, a notice and the grounds provided by law. Proceedings not based on the correct ground and procedure may be dismissed.

In which cases is an action for annulment of a title deed brought?

An action for annulment and re-registration of a title deed may arise in cases such as fraud, simulation (muvazaa) or abuse of a power of attorney.

What can I do if the expropriation price is too low?

Determination of the real value may be requested by an action for the determination of the price.
