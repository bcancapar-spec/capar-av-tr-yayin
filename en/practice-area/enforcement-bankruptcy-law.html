This page explains Turkish law. It is an English version of the Turkish page; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

Protection against unjustified enforcement is as important as collecting a receivable. Acting for creditors, the office pursues effective collection; acting for debtors, it defends against unlawful enforcement proceedings.

Strategic representation is provided in enforcement proceedings (icra takibi), attachment, actions for annulment or removal of objections, negative declaratory actions and bankruptcy proceedings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Enforcement proceedings have been started against me. What should I do?

An objection must be made in time after the payment order is served; otherwise the proceedings become final. The period is very short.

How do I collect my receivable?

Receivables based on promissory notes, cheques, invoices or contracts can be pursued through enforcement proceedings; if an objection is made, the process continues with an action for annulment of the objection.

What if enforcement was started although I owe nothing?

A negative declaratory action may be brought to establish that no debt is owed.
