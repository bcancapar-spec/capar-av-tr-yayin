Every stage of commercial life, from the formation of a company to relations between shareholders and from contracts to the resolution of disputes, calls for sound legal structuring. The office advises and represents individual entrepreneurs and companies in Denizli and the surrounding region in commercial and corporate law.

In the formation of limited and joint-stock companies, the drafting of articles of association, disputes between shareholders, share transfers, the drafting of commercial contracts and the conduct of commercial cases, the office follows a strategic approach that puts the client's commercial interests first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a limited company formed in Denizli?

A limited company is formed by drawing up the articles of association, committing the capital, registering with the trade registry and completing the necessary permits and registrations. The process is planned according to the shareholding structure and the field of activity.

How is a dispute between shareholders resolved?

The articles of association and the rights attached to the shares are examined first; a solution may be reached through settlement and negotiation or, where necessary, through actions for withdrawal or exclusion from the company or for its dissolution.

Should I consult a lawyer before signing a commercial contract?

Yes. Reviewing the contract in advance largely prevents disputes and losses that may arise later; it is the most effective step in risk management.
