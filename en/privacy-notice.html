Privacy Notice (KVKK Information Notice)

This page is the English version of the Turkish original; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

1. Data Controller

Av. Bayram Can ÇAPAR - ÇAPAR HUKUK BÜROSU acts as the data controller within the meaning of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu, “KVKK”). This notice has been prepared to fulfil the duty to inform regarding the personal data processed through the capar.av.tr website.

2. Data Processed and Method of Collection

Through the meeting/appointment form on the site, your name and surname, e-mail address, telephone number, the subject and content of your message, and technical data relating to the connection (IP address, date of the request) are collected by automated means. The site also uses a limited number of cookies and similar technologies for functionality and security purposes (see the Cookie Policy).

3. Purposes of Processing

Your personal data are processed for the purposes of receiving and assessing your request, contacting you, conducting the meeting/appointment process, establishing a legal advisory relationship and ensuring the security of the site. Your data are not used for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Grounds

Your data are processed on the legal grounds that processing is necessary to fulfil your request, your explicit consent and the legitimate interest of the data controller.

5. Transfers

Your personal data are not shared with third parties, without prejudice to public institutions and organisations authorised by law. Information within the scope of the attorney–client relationship is additionally protected as professional secrecy under Article 36 of the Attorneys’ Act No. 1136 (Avukatlık Kanunu).

6. Retention Period

The data are kept for the period required by the relevant purpose and provided for in the legislation; at the end of that period they are erased, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Rights of the Data Subject (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your data are processed; to request information; to learn the purpose of processing; to request correction if they are incomplete or inaccurate; to request erasure or destruction where the conditions are met; to object to a result to your detriment arising from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage. You may send your requests to info@capar.av.tr.
