Frequently Asked Legal Questions

From whom can I claim diminished vehicle value after a traffic accident?

You may claim it from the compulsory motor liability (traffic) insurer of the party at fault and, where necessary, from the driver at fault. Usually a written application is made to the insurer first; if no result is obtained, the Insurance Arbitration Commission or the courts may be approached.

Which court has jurisdiction over work accident compensation claims?

As a rule, the labor courts have jurisdiction over actions for pecuniary and non-pecuniary damages arising from a work accident. The process includes technical stages such as the determination of fault, the degree of incapacity and an actuarial (qualified) calculation.

How is property divided on divorce?

Unless the spouses have agreed otherwise, the regime of participation in acquired property applies. A participation claim arises for half of the property acquired during the marriage (the net surplus); personal property is, as a rule, not divided.

How long is the period for objecting to enforcement proceedings?

In enforcement proceedings without a court judgment, an objection must be made to the enforcement office within the period provided by law from the service of the payment order. As missing the period can make the proceedings final, attention should be paid to the date of service.

Where should a consumer dispute be brought?

Consumer arbitration committees have jurisdiction over disputes below a certain monetary threshold, and consumer courts over disputes above it. The threshold is updated every year.
