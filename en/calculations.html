Calculations

Information tools that work according to the general rules of Turkish law: each calculation is made under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye) and shows its statutory basis. The calculations run in your browser; the information you enter is not sent to the server and is not stored.

  • Sentence execution calculator
  • Employee receivables calculator

  • Inheritance share calculator
  • Court and enforcement fee calculator
    • Court fee calculator
    • Enforcement fee calculator
  • Retirement calculator
  • Lease termination calculator
  • Limitation period calculator
  • Import duty (HS code) calculator
  • Customs procedures calculator

How to use: open the calculator that fits your situation, enter the information requested and press the calculate button; the result is shown on the same page, together with how it was reached.

Results are preliminary estimates. Information tool; not legal advice. The results should be compared with the relevant statutory text and the documents in the file. Each calculator is the English version of a Turkish original and uses the same rules and the same calculation engine; the Turkish version prevails.

  • Sentence Execution Calculator

    For a final prison sentence, calculates the dates of conditional release (koşullu salıverilme), probation (denetimli serbestlik), transfer to an open penal institution and release under Law No. 5275.

    For: convicts, their relatives and anyone who wants to know the execution dates.

    Information you will need

    • Offence and date of the offence in the judgment
    • Sentence imposed (years, months, days)
    • Date of birth of the convict
    • Start of execution (date of arrest or surrender)

    6 required fields (4 more for each additional judgment); the other sections are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Conditional release (5275 art. 107, art. 108)
    • Probation (5275 art. 105/A)
    • Open penal institution (5275 art. 14)
    • Release on completion of the sentence (TCK art. 61, art. 63)
    • Supervision period and loss of rights (5275 art. 107; TCK art. 53)
    Calculate the execution dates →

    Turkish original: İnfaz Hesaplama

  • Employee Receivables Calculator

    Calculates the severance pay (kıdem tazminatı) and notice pay (ihbar tazminatı) of an employee who was dismissed or left, and receivables such as unpaid wages, annual leave and overtime, gross and estimated net.

    For: employees who were dismissed or left their job, and employers.

    Information you will need

    • Date of starting work and last working day
    • Last monthly gross wage on the payslip
    • How the contract ended
    • Regular payments such as travel, meals or bonuses, if any

    4 required fields; other receivables and the gross-to-net section are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Severance pay (1475 art. 14)
    • Notice pay and bad-faith compensation (4857 art. 17)
    • Consequences of reinstatement (4857 art. 21)
    • Overtime, weekly rest and public holidays (4857 art. 41, 46, 47)
    • Annual leave pay (4857 art. 53, 59)
    • Gross-to-net deductions
    Calculate the employee receivables →

    Turkish original: İşçilik Alacakları Hesaplama

  • Bodily Injury Compensation Calculator

    Estimates the pecuniary compensation of a person permanently injured in a work accident, a traffic accident or another event. Loss-of-support compensation in fatal accidents and non-pecuniary damages are not calculated.

    For: people injured in a work or traffic accident, and their relatives.

    Information you will need

    • Date of birth and date of the accident
    • Incapacity rate (permanent incapacity percentage; stated in the SGK, Forensic Medicine or university hospital report)
    • Fault rate
    • Monthly net wage on the accident date (if left empty, the minimum wage is used)

    7 required fields (a few more questions for traffic accidents and other injuries); SGK payments are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Remaining life expectancy (TRH 2010; PMF 1931 comparison)
    • Known and unknown periods
    • Active and passive periods
    • Reduction for fault (TBK art. 52; KTK art. 86)
    • SGK set-off (TBK art. 55; 5510 art. 21)
    • Traffic insurance limit (KTK art. 91)
    Calculate the bodily injury compensation →

    Turkish original: Bedensel Zarar Tazminatı Hesaplama

  • Inheritance Share Calculator

    Calculates who inherits and the statutory share and reserved portion of each heir as a fraction and a percentage, and in TL if amounts are entered; shows the disposable portion and whether dispositions such as a will exceed it.

    For: heirs, people considering a will or an inheritance contract, and anyone who wants to know how an estate is shared.

    Information you will need

    • Date of death
    • Status of the surviving spouse and the children (alive, died earlier, disclaimed …)
    • If there are no children, the mother, father and siblings; failing them, the grandparents
    • Optional: estate, debts, gifts and amounts left by will

    2 required fields (the mother and father if there are no children, the grandparents if there are none of them either); amounts are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Statutory heirs and groups (TMK art. 495–497)
    • Share of the surviving spouse (TMK art. 499)
    • Disclaimer, disinheritance, unworthiness (TMK art. 511, 579, 611–613)
    • Reserved portion and disposable portion (TMK art. 505, 506)
    • Calculation estate (TMK art. 507, 508)
    Calculate the inheritance shares →

    Turkish original: Miras Payı ve Saklı Pay Hesaplama

  • Court Fee Calculator

    Calculates, item by item with the 2026 tariff, the filing fee, the advance judgment fee (one quarter; one twentieth in claims for death or bodily injury), the power of attorney fee and the advance on costs payable when a case is filed; shows the balance after judgment, the fee on an amendment or increase of the claim and appeal fees.

    For: people filing a case, increasing the value of their claim or lodging an appeal.

    Information you will need

    • Date of filing or application
    • Court and type of case (proportional or fixed)
    • Value of the claim; for an amendment, the increased amount and its date
    • Optional: number of parties, notification fee, bar stamp

    3 required fields (type of calculation, date, court; the value in a proportional case); costs are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Filing fee (Tariff (1) A-I)
    • Judgment and writ fee, advance and balance (Fees Law art. 28/a; Tariff A-III)
    • Fee on amendment or increase of the claim (Fees Law art. 30, 32)
    • Appeal fees (Tariff A-IV)
    • Power of attorney fee and advance on costs (Tariff D-I-c; Code of Civil Procedure art. 120)
    Calculate the court fee →

    Turkish original: Dava Harcı Hesaplama

  • Enforcement Fee Calculator

    Calculates, item by item with the 2026 tariff, the filing fee, the advance fee of 5 per thousand and the power of attorney fee payable when enforcement proceedings are started; the collection fee by stage of payment or collection, the set-off of the advance fee and the prison construction fee; also covers eviction and delivery of movables.

    For: creditors starting enforcement proceedings, debtors about to pay and anyone who wants to know the costs of enforcement.

    Information you will need

    • Date of the proceedings
    • Type of proceedings (without a judgment, negotiable instrument, pledge, judgment, eviction …)
    • Amount claimed or, for eviction, the annual rent
    • Optional: stage of collection, amount collected, bar stamp

    3 required fields (date, type of proceedings, amount); the stage and costs are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Enforcement filing fee (Tariff (1) B-I-1)
    • Advance fee and its set-off (Fees Law art. 29)
    • Collection fee (Tariff B-I-3)
    • Prison construction fee (Law No. 2548 art. 1)
    • Eviction and delivery fee (Fees Law art. 18, 19)
    Calculate the enforcement fee →

    Turkish original: İcra Harcı Hesaplama

  • Retirement Eligibility Calculator

    Determines the insurance period, premium day and age conditions for an old-age or retirement pension according to the date of first insurance; shows what is missing and the earliest date for the full pension and for a pension with fewer premium days. Takes into account the age condition removed by Law No. 7438 for entries on or before 8 September 1999, military service and childbirth credits and service in more than one status.

    For: 4/a (SSK), 4/b (Bağ-Kur) and 4/c (Retirement Fund) insured persons who want to know their retirement date.

    Information you will need

    • Sex and date of birth
    • Status and date of first insurance
    • Total premium days (SGK service record on e-Devlet)
    • Optional: military service and childbirth credits, loss of working capacity

    5 required fields (sex, date of birth, status, first entry, premium days); credits are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Entry on or before 8 September 1999 (Law No. 506 provisional art. 81; Law No. 1479 provisional art. 10; Law No. 5434 provisional art. 205; Law No. 5510 provisional art. 95)
    • Entry 9 September 1999 – 30 April 2008 (Law No. 5510 provisional art. 9)
    • Entry on or after 1 May 2008 (Law No. 5510 art. 28)
    • Credits and the age-18 rule (Law No. 5510 art. 38, 41)
    • More than one status (repealed Law No. 2829 art. 8; Law No. 5510 art. 53)
    Calculate the retirement date →

    Turkish original: Ne Zaman Emekli Olurum? Emeklilik Hesaplama

  • Lease Termination Calculator

    Shows on a timeline, with the legal basis of each date, the last day a termination notice must arrive, the day the lease ends and the last day for an eviction action or enforcement in leases of dwellings, roofed business premises, other immovables, movables and usufructuary leases; states mandatory mediation and the competent court.

    For: tenants, landlords, buyers of leased property and heirs who want to end a lease.

    Information you will need

    • Type of lease and its term
    • Start date of the lease
    • Party ending the lease and the ground
    • Date of the notice, warning, acquisition or undertaking

    5 required fields (type, term, start date, party and ground, date for the ground); the notice period in the contract is optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Ordinary termination and termination periods (Code of Obligations art. 327–330, 347, 367, 368)
    • Eviction actions and extension of the time to sue (art. 350–353)
    • Default, duty of care, good cause, death of the tenant (art. 315, 316, 331, 333, 362, 369)
    • Last day falling on a holiday (art. 93; Code of Civil Procedure art. 93)
    • Mandatory mediation and competent court (Law No. 6325 art. 18/B; Code of Civil Procedure art. 4)
    Calculate the termination dates →

    Turkish original: Kira Sözleşmesi Feshi Hesaplama

  • Limitation Period Calculator

    Shows, by type of claim or action, whether the time limit is a limitation period or a preclusive period, its length, start and last day with the legal basis. Takes into account mediation, the pandemic suspension, interruption by acknowledgement, part payment, action or enforcement, and a last day falling on a holiday.

    For: anyone who wants to know whether a claim can still be pursued or the last day to bring an action.

    Information you will need

    • Type of claim or action
    • Starting date (due date, knowledge, delivery, service …)
    • Optional: mediation and other suspension periods, acknowledgement, payment, action or enforcement date

    3 required fields (area, type, starting date); suspension and interruption events are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • General and five-year limitation, tort, unjust enrichment (TBK art. 72, 82, 146, 147)
    • Defects, consumers, insurance, traffic accidents, cheques, promissory notes, carriage (TBK art. 231, 244, 478; Law No. 6502 art. 12, 16; TTK art. 749, 814, 855, 1420; KTK art. 109)
    • Employment claims and reinstatement (Law No. 4857 art. 20, 32, additional art. 3)
    • Inheritance, pre-emption, possession, divorce (TMK art. 178, 559, 571, 606, 639, 733, 984)
    • Enforcement: judgment-based enforcement, annulment of objection, restitution (İİK art. 39, 67, 72)
    Calculate the last day →

    Turkish original: Zamanaşımı ve Hak Düşürücü Süre Hesaplama

  • Import Duty Calculator

    Calculates the customs value and, along the tax base chain, customs duty, additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, KKDF, stamp duty, ÖTV and VAT line by line from the tariff code, origin and CIF value. For exports it shows the destination tax burden and the exporters' association levy; for parcels and travellers the flat-rate tax. The country panel shows the trade regime with Türkiye and the documents needed.

    For: importers, exporters and anyone receiving parcels or bringing goods from abroad.

    Information you will need

    • What you do (import, export, parcel, traveller) and the country
    • Invoice amount, currency and exchange rate; freight, insurance, domestic costs
    • Rates from the official tariff query (customs duty, ÖTV, VAT; additional duty, anti-dumping, KKDF if any)

    For imports 4 required fields (invoice, exchange rate, customs duty rate, VAT rate); other taxes and costs are optional.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Customs value, exchange rate and tax base chain (Customs Law art. 24, 27, 28, 30; VAT Law art. 21; ÖTV Law art. 11)
    • Ad valorem, specific, mixed and quota duties; preferential / non-preferential comparison
    • Parcels and travellers' goods (Decision No. 2009/15481 art. 45, 61–63)
    • Exports: VAT and stamp duty exemption, exporters' association levy (Law No. 3065 art. 11; Law No. 5910 art. 18)
    • Country panel: Customs Union, free and preferential trade agreements; A.TR, EUR.1, certificate of origin (Law No. 5174 art. 26)
    Calculate the taxes →

    Turkish original: GTİP ve İthalat Vergileri Hesaplama

  • Customs Procedures Calculator

    Calculates Turkish customs penalties, objection, settlement and action deadlines, interest at the late-payment rate, storage and liquidation periods, penalties under temporary import and inward processing, compensatory duty, outward processing, the origin percentage rule, customs value adjustments and the repayment of duties.

    For: importers, exporters and their advisers who want to check a customs penalty, a deadline or a duty calculation.

    Information you will need

    • What you would like to calculate (penalty, deadline, interest, procedure, origin, value, repayment)
    • Amounts: duties, duty-paid value, invoice price
    • Dates: notification, registration of the declaration, detection

    1 required choice and 2–4 required fields depending on the module.

    Calculated items and their legal basis
    • Penalties: loss of duties, prohibited goods, warehouses, procedure breaches, irregularities (art. 234–238, 241)
    • Objection, settlement, deemed rejection and action deadlines (art. 242, 244; Law No. 2577 art. 7, 8)
    • Temporary import, inward and outward processing, compensatory duty (art. 133, 141, 194, 238)
    • Origin percentage rule and tolerance (Pan-Euro-Mediterranean Decision 1/2023); customs value (art. 27, 28)
    • Repayment of duties and interest (art. 211, 213, 214, 216)
    Calculate →

    Turkish original: Gümrük İşlemleri Hesaplama
