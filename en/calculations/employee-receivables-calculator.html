Employee Receivables Calculator

Go to the calculator

Calculates, under Turkish law, severance pay (kıdem tazminatı) and notice pay (ihbar tazminatı) on the gross wage including regular benefits; bad-faith, reinstatement, discrimination and trade-union compensation and pay for the remaining term; and unpaid wages, annual leave, overtime, weekly rest and general holiday receivables. Each item is shown with its legal basis and step-by-step calculation, and the deductions from gross to net in a table. For periods without data, no amount is given, and the tool says so.

Short answer: Under Turkish law, severance pay (kıdem tazminatı), notice pay (ihbar tazminatı) and annual leave pay are calculated from the last wage, and overtime, weekly rest and general holiday receivables from the wage of the relevant period. Severance pay is 30 days' gross wage including regular benefits for each full year and cannot exceed the ceiling on the date of termination (1475 art. 14); notice pay is 2 to 8 weeks' gross wage including regular benefits, depending on the length of service (4857 art. 17); the other wage receivables are calculated from the bare wage. Only stamp duty is deducted from severance pay, income tax and stamp duty from notice pay, and, in addition, social insurance and unemployment premiums from wage receivables.

Information tool; not legal advice.

How to use: fill in the required fields, open the optional sections that fit your situation and press the calculate button; the result appears on this page, just below the form. Results are preliminary estimates.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required fields: date of starting work, last working day, way in which the contract ended and last monthly bare gross wage. The other sections are optional. Enter amounts in the Turkish format, with a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals: write “33.030,00” (33,030.00), “33030” or “33030.50”; results are shown as 1,234.56 (TL = Turkish lira) and dates as DD.MM.YYYY. No personal data, file number or employer name is requested.

1. Employment relationship

The first day of work in the employment contract or in the SGK service record.

The last day is included in the length of service. If the notice period was worked, enter the last day of that period.

Choose the option that matches who ended the contract and why.

Used to check job security (4857 art. 18/1) for bad-faith compensation and the consequences of reinstatement.

Used for the twenty-day minimum annual leave (4857 art. 53) for employees aged eighteen or younger or fifty or older.

2. Wage

The monthly gross wage on the payslip; regular payments such as travel, meals and bonuses are added separately below. Do not enter the net take-home amount: the tool treats every amount as gross, so a net figure makes all receivables too low.

For the gross wage including regular benefits (giydirilmiş brüt ücret), add separately each payment in money or benefit measurable in money that is paid regularly and continuously (such as travel, meals, bonus, premium, fuel allowance). Do not add one-off occasional payments. Annual payments are divided by 365 days and quarterly payments by 90 days and added to the 30-day wage. For meal and travel allowances paid per working day, enter the number of days for which they are paid in a month: 26 (default) for a six-day week, 22 for a five-day week.

3. Other receivables (optional)

Severance pay and notice pay are always checked. Tick the box for the other items.

Unpaid wages and annual leave

If unused days are left empty, the leave earned is calculated for each full year of service with the minimum periods in 4857 art. 53; for longer periods under the contract or a collective labour agreement, enter the unused days.

Overtime, weekly rest, general holidays

For each period, enter the number of hours exceeding 45 hours a week. The hourly wage is the monthly bare wage divided by 225. Enter days not worked, such as leave and sick leave, as “excluded days”.

The equitable reduction (hakkaniyet indirimi) applies only to the part proved by witness statements; the Court of Cassation General Assembly of Civil Chambers found a thirty per cent rate appropriate (Yargıtay HGK 2015/1616 E., 2016/128 K.). The rate is at the court's discretion.

Other compensation arising from termination

Only one of bad-faith, discrimination and trade-union compensation can be claimed for the same termination; if more than one is selected, the higher amount is calculated and the amounts of the others are shown but not added to the total.

4. Gross to net (optional): payment date, tax base, minimum wage exemption

If left empty, the last working day is used. The income tax tariff and the rates of the year of this date apply.

Enter the cumulative tax base from the payroll; if left empty, the calculation starts from zero (the first bracket) and the net amount may appear higher than it is.

If you would like to assess the result together with your own situation, see the Labor & Social Security Law page or the Contact page.

Turkish law framework

This calculator applies Turkish labour law, i.e. it works under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye): Labour Act No. 4857, Article 14 of the former Labour Act No. 1475 (still in force for severance pay), Law No. 6356 on Trade Unions and Collective Labour Agreements, the Turkish Code of Obligations (TBK, Law No. 6098), Law No. 5510 on Social Insurance and General Health Insurance, Law No. 4447 on Unemployment Insurance, the Income Tax Law No. 193 and the Stamp Duty Law No. 488. The Turkish version is the original: İşçilik Alacakları Hesaplama.

Gross wage including regular benefits

In calculating the wage on which severance pay is based, “para ve para ile ölçülmesi mümkün akdi ve kanundan doğan menfaatler de gözönünde tutulur” (1475 art. 14/11; translation: “benefits in money and measurable in money arising from the contract and from the law are also taken into account”); the same rule appears for notice pay and bad-faith compensation in the last paragraph of 4857 art. 17. The Court of Cassation General Assembly of Civil Chambers summarises the distinction as follows: “kıdem tazminatı, giydirilmiş ücretten hesaplanırken, diğer işçilik alacakları (fazla çalışma, hafta tatili, yıllık ücretli izin alacakları gibi) çıplak ücretten hesaplanmaktadır” (Yargıtay HGK 2016/2084 E., 2020/20 K.; translation: “while severance pay is calculated from the gross wage including regular benefits, the other employee receivables (such as overtime, weekly rest and annual paid leave receivables) are calculated from the bare wage”). Occasional payments that are not continuous are not included (Yargıtay 7. HD 2015/6784 E., 2016/7198 K.). For payments made once a year or not periodically, the annual amount is divided by 365 days, and for periodic payments the amount for the last period is divided by the number of days in the period (Yargıtay 9. HD 2016/24123 E., 2020/5415 K.); the tool converts this share into 30 days and adds it to the bare wage. For meal and travel allowances paid per working day, the Court of Cassation takes the number of days actually worked in a month: 26 days for a six-day week (Yargıtay 22. HD 2015/33111 E., 2018/7814 K.) and 22 days for a five-day week (Yargıtay 7. HD 2014/21982 E., 2015/23951 K.); the tool multiplies the daily amount by the number of days entered, the default being 26. The minimum living allowance (asgari geçim indirimi) is not added to the gross wage including regular benefits (same decision).

  • Income tax: calculated progressively with the wage tariff of the payment year, on top of the cumulative tax base entered (Law No. 193 art. 103). The tax base is the gross amount less the employee shares of the social insurance and unemployment premiums.
  • Stamp duty: 7.59 per thousand on “Maaş, ücret, gündelik, … tazminat ve benzeri her ne adla olursa olsun hizmet karşılığı alınan paralar” (Law No. 488 table (1) IV-1-b; translation: “salaries, wages, daily pay, … compensation and similar sums received for services, under whatever name”).
  • Minimum wage exemption (2022 onwards): no tax is levied on the part of the wage corresponding to the gross minimum wage less the employee premiums, and no stamp duty on the part corresponding to the monthly gross minimum wage (Law No. 193 art. 23/1-18; Law No. 488 table (2) IV-34). The exemption is applied once to the total of the wage payments made in the month of payment and “asgari ücretin ilgili ayda hesaplanan vergisini geçemeyecektir” (Income Tax General Communiqué (GV Genel Tebliği) Serial No: 319 art. 4/5, art. 6/1; translation: “cannot exceed the tax calculated on the minimum wage for the relevant month”). The tool finds the minimum wage tax for that month on the minimum wage tax base from the start of the year up to that month. Since severance pay is compensation and not a wage, the stamp duty exemption does not apply to it.
  • Not taken into account: the ceiling on earnings subject to premiums (5510 art. 82), the minimum living allowance for payments before 2022, the disability allowance and wages from more than one employer.

Order of calculation

  1. Period: the length of service is found in years, months and days between the date of starting work and the last working day (inclusive): the full calendar months completed from the starting date up to the day after the last day (twelve of them make a year) and the calendar days remaining after the last monthly anniversary are counted; where the anniversary day does not exist in that month (e.g. 31 August + 6 months, 29 February + 1 year), the first day of the following month is taken.
  2. Wage: the gross wage including regular benefits is found by adding to the bare wage the 30-day share of each regular payment (for payments per working day, the daily amount × the days paid per month).
  3. Items: each item is calculated on its own wage base; amounts are calculated as whole numbers of kuruş, and each intermediate result is rounded half a kuruş up.
  4. Equitable reduction: the rate entered is deducted from the total of overtime, weekly rest and general holiday pay.
  5. Deductions: social insurance and unemployment premiums, income tax (cumulative) and stamp duty; if selected, the minimum wage exemption is deducted from the total.

Not calculated

  • Limitation periods: five years for wage receivables (4857 art. 32); five years for severance pay, notice pay, bad-faith compensation, compensation for breach of equal treatment and annual leave pay (4857 additional art. 3). If the payment date is more than five years after the last working day, the tool warns but does not cut the amount. Releases (ibraname), set-offs and earlier payments; balancing, compensatory work and time off in lieu for overtime.
  • Mediation: in actions for employee receivables and compensation based on the law, an individual or a collective labour agreement, and in reinstatement actions, prior application to a mediator is a condition of the action; limitation is suspended from the application until the final report is drawn up (Labour Courts Law No. 7036 art. 3/1, 3/17).
  • Automatic expiry of a fixed-term contract, termination by mutual agreement (ikale), transfer of a workplace and cases with more than one employer; part-time, on-call and piece work; seasonal and campaign work lasting less than a year (the annual leave provisions do not apply: 4857 art. 53/3); employees subject to the Maritime Labour Law No. 854 and the Press Labour Law No. 5953.
  • Higher rates under a collective labour agreement or an employment contract, night work and the percentage system. In underground work annual leave is increased by four days and the six-month seniority condition for job security does not apply (4857 art. 53/4, art. 18/1): enter the increased leave in the "unused days" field. Work exceeding 7.5 hours a day on a weekly rest day or a holiday must be entered separately as overtime.
  • Periods in which the contract was suspended: periods not worked, such as unpaid leave, strike and lock-out or detention, are deducted from the length of service and leave entitlement; the tool does not deduct these periods. In a termination where the wage for the notice period is paid in advance, the notice period is not added to seniority; if the notice period was worked, enter its last day as the last working day.
  • The amount of interest, lawyer's fees and court costs; discretionary compensation under TBK art. 438/3 and non-pecuniary damages.
  • No personal data, file number or employer name is requested; the calculation is made in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the gross wage including regular benefits (giydirilmiş brüt ücret), and for which receivables is it used?

It is found by adding to the bare gross wage the money and benefits measurable in money that are paid regularly and continuously, such as travel, meals, bonuses, premiums and fuel allowance; one-off occasional payments are not included. Meal and travel allowances paid per working day are converted into a monthly amount by multiplying them by the number of days for which they are paid in a month. Severance pay, notice pay and bad-faith compensation are calculated from this wage; annual leave pay from the last bare wage; overtime, weekly rest and general holiday receivables from the bare wage of the relevant period. Legal basis: 1475 art. 14/11; 4857 art. 17/last paragraph; Yargıtay HGK 2016/2084 E., 2020/20 K.

How is severance pay (kıdem tazminatı) calculated?

For each full year of service, 30 days' gross wage including regular benefits is paid; the remaining months and days are added pro rata. The wage taken as the basis cannot exceed the severance pay ceiling on the date of termination. Only stamp duty at 7.59 per thousand is deducted from severance pay. Legal basis: 1475 art. 14/1 and 14/11; Law No. 193 art. 25/7; Law No. 488 table (1) IV-1-b.

In which cases is a person entitled to severance pay?

Severance pay arises for an employee who has worked for at least one year when the employer terminates the contract for a reason other than conduct contrary to morality and good faith, when the employee terminates for just cause, on leaving for military service or for a retirement or old-age pension, on leaving after completing the retirement conditions other than age (insurance period and number of premium days), when a female employee leaves within one year of marriage, and on the employee's death; it does not arise on a resignation without just cause. Legal basis: 1475 art. 14/1.

How long are the notice periods?

Two weeks for an employee whose employment lasted less than six months, four weeks from six months to one and a half years, six weeks from one and a half to three years, and eight weeks for an employee whose employment lasted more than three years. The party that does not comply with the notice requirement pays compensation equal to the wage for this period; the amount is calculated from the gross wage including regular benefits. Legal basis: 4857 art. 17/2, 17/4 and 17/last paragraph.

How is overtime pay (fazla çalışma ücreti) calculated?

For each hour exceeding forty-five hours a week, the hourly wage plus fifty per cent is paid; if the weekly hours are set lower by contract, work up to forty-five hours is work for extended hours and is paid with a twenty-five per cent increase. The tool finds the hourly wage by dividing the monthly bare wage by 225. Legal basis: 4857 art. 41/1-3.

Which deductions are made from the gross amount?

From receivables in the nature of wages: the 14 per cent employee share of the social insurance premium, the 1 per cent unemployment insurance premium, income tax and stamp duty; from notice pay, income tax and stamp duty; from severance pay, only stamp duty. Bad-faith, non-reinstatement, discrimination, trade-union and remaining-term compensation are shown gross. Legal basis: 5510 art. 80 and art. 81; 4447 art. 49; Law No. 193 art. 25, art. 61 and art. 103; Law No. 488 table (1) IV-1-b.
