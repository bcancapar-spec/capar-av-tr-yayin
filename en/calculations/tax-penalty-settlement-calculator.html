Tax Penalty and Settlement Calculator under Turkish Law: Tax Loss Penalty, Art. 376 Reduction, Settlement, Action and Request for Explanation (2026)

Go to the calculator

If a tax and penalty notice has been served, enter the tax and penalty type on the notice: the calculator shows the tax loss penalty (with late filing, unregistered activity and repeat offence), late-payment interest and the amount and last day of four routes side by side under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye). It does not tell you which route to take; it compares amounts and deadlines only.

Short answer: Under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), the tax loss penalty equals the tax lost; it is threefold for acts under art. 359 and single for participants (Tax Procedure Law art. 344). If you apply within 30 days of service and pay the tax and half of the penalty on time, half of the penalty is waived (art. 376). Settlement can be requested within the same 30 days; post-assessment settlement covers penalties only, and threefold and participation penalties are excluded (add. art. 1). An action is filed within 30 days and suspends collection, while late-payment interest runs until service of the judgment.

Information tool; not legal advice. Information only; it does not replace a tax return, the work of a certified accountant or legal advice (Laws No. 3568 and 1136). The tax office sets the final amount of tax, penalty and interest.

How to use: enter the amounts and dates requested and press the calculate button. Amounts can be written as 125,000.50. The result appears on this page, just below the form.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

How the calculation works

  1. Penalty: Tax loss penalty = tax × 1 (× 3 for acts under art. 359, × 1 for participants); half for a late return filed of own motion; 50% more for activity unknown to the tax office (art. 344).
  2. Repeat offence: If an earlier tax loss penalty became final and the new penalty is imposed before the end of the calendar year in which the fifth year from the day after finality ends, the penalty rises by 50%; the increase cannot exceed the final penalty (art. 339).
  3. Late-payment interest: Whole months from the normal due date to the end date at the surcharge rate; part months are dropped (art. 112/3). If no end date is entered, the date of service is used.
  4. Article 376: Half of the penalty is waived; the tax and the remaining half are paid. Apply within 30 days of service (last day under art. 18).
  5. Settlement: Penalties only; the penalty is reduced by your example rate, tax and interest stay the same. Not available for threefold and participation penalties (add. art. 1).
  6. Action: 30 days; filing suspends collection; if lost, the tax, the full penalty and interest until service of the judgment are paid (Law No. 2577 art. 7, 27/3; art. 112/3).
  7. Request for explanation: If the explanation is found insufficient and the return and payment with surcharge are made within 30 days of the evaluation letter, the penalty is 20% of the tax; the surcharge counts each month and part month, part months daily (Council of State 3rd Chamber 2025/5922), with the tax office whole-month figure shown too (art. 370). For a threefold penalty the route exists only under art. 370/b; no amount is calculated.

Not covered

  • Irregularity and special irregularity penalties (art. 352–355): second wave; the 50% higher art. 376 reduction for irregularities up to 40,000 TL is not calculated.
  • Pre-assessment settlement (add. art. 11): shown as a note only.
  • The settlement commission's reduction cannot be predicted: the amount uses your example rate only.
  • Which route to take: the calculator gives no recommendation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the tax loss penalty calculated in Türkiye?

It equals the tax lost, threefold for acts listed in art. 359 (false documents etc.) and single for participants (Law No. 213 art. 344/1-2); for a return filed late of the taxpayer's own motion before an audit the penalty is 50%, and for activity without registration it is increased by 50%.

What is the article 376 reduction?

If within 30 days of service the taxpayer applies to the tax office and undertakes to pay the tax and half of the penalty on the due date (with security, within three months), half of the penalty is waived; if payment is not made or an action is filed, the reduction is lost (Law No. 213 art. 376).

Can the tax itself be reduced in settlement?

No: since Law No. 7524, post-assessment settlement covers only tax loss penalties and irregularity penalties above 40,000 TL in 2026, and penalties for acts under art. 359, participation penalties and penalties under art. 370/b are excluded (Law No. 213 add. art. 1).

How much does a repeat offence increase the penalty?

If a new tax loss penalty is imposed before the end of the calendar year in which the fifth year from the day after finality ends, the penalty rises by 50%, but the increase cannot exceed the final penalty (Law No. 213 art. 339); the Council of State applies this cap as the more favourable law (Tax Chambers Council E.2021/1448, K.2023/72; 9th Chamber E.2023/1904, K.2025/2991).

What happens if I file an action?

Filing a tax action suspends collection of the tax and penalty in dispute (İYUK, Law No. 2577 art. 27/3); if it is lost, the tax, the full penalty and interest from the normal due date to service of the judgment are paid (Law No. 213 art. 112/3-b), and the art. 376 reduction is lost.

What is the penalty under a request for explanation?

If the explanation is found insufficient and within 30 days the return is filed and the tax is paid with a surcharge for each month and part month, the tax loss penalty is 20% of the tax (Law No. 213 art. 370/a-2); for false or misleading documents this is limited to amounts up to 870,000 TL in 2026 or 5% of purchases (art. 370/b).
