Property Capital Gains Tax Calculator for Türkiye: Five-Year Rule, Indexation, Exemption and Income Tax

Go to the calculator

Enter how and when you acquired the property, the sale date and the prices: under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye) the calculator checks whether the sale is within the five-year (four for acquisitions before 2007) period, indexes the cost, deducts the exemption and shows the income tax by bracket with the return and instalments (Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 80, 81, 103).

Short answer: How it works under Turkish law: if you sell real estate acquired for consideration within five years of its registration in the land register, the gain is a capital gain; property acquired by inheritance or gift is not taxed on sale. The cost is indexed if the index increase is 10% or more, sale costs are deducted, 150,000 TL of the gain is exempt in 2026 and the rest is taxed at the income tax rates and declared in March of the following year (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 80, 81, 103).

Information tool; not legal advice. It does not replace a tax return, an accountant's work or legal advice: if you enter a wrong value, the result is wrong.

How to use: answer the questions from top to bottom, enter the amounts and press the calculate button. The result appears on this page, just below the form, with a detailed report and a PDF.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

1. Acquisition and sale
How did you acquire the property?

For a cooperative home the allocation date; for a unit for land, the unit's registration date.

2. Prices

Purchase price; you may add costs forming part of the cost, such as the title deed fee paid on purchase.

For example the title deed fee you paid as seller; leave empty if none.

3. Index (optional)

The law refers to the wholesale price index of the State Institute of Statistics (today TÜİK); in practice TÜİK's domestic producer price index (Yİ-ÜFE) is used instead, and the basis of this substitution was not separately verified here. Enter the values of the month before acquisition and the month before sale from the TÜİK table; the calculator holds no index values (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 81).

4. Other income in the same year (optional)

The exemption is annual, so gains are added together.

For example rental income you will declare; the tax is calculated as the part added on top.

How the calculation works

  1. Scope: a sale within five years of acquisition of property acquired for consideration; property acquired by inheritance or gift is outside the scope (duplicate art. 80/1-6). For acquisitions before 01.01.2007 the period is four years (provisional art. 71).
  2. Dates: the land register registration dates for acquisition and sale; for a cooperative the allocation date; for a unit for land the unit's registration date.
  3. Net gain: the cost and the sale costs, taxes and fees borne by you are deducted from the sale price; the cost is indexed if the index increase is 10% or more (duplicate art. 81).
  4. Exemption and tax: the annual exemption is deducted and the rest is taxed bracket by bracket at the income tax rates (art. 103).
  5. Filing and payment: return between 1 and 25 March of the following year; two instalments in March and July (art. 92, art. 117).

Frequently asked questions

When does capital gains tax arise on selling a home in Türkiye?

If real estate acquired for consideration is sold within five years of acquisition, the gain is taxed; for property acquired before 01.01.2007 the period is four years. A sale of property acquired by inheritance or gift gives no capital gain (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 80/1-6, provisional art. 71).

From which date does the five-year period run?

The period runs from the registration of the property in the land register; for a cooperative home, from the allocation date. An independent unit received in exchange for land is a new acquisition separate from the land, and the period runs from the unit's registration (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 80/1-6; Council of State, Tax Chambers Board E.2021/1448, K.2023/72).

How is the cost indexed?

The cost is increased by the index increase excluding the month of disposal; if the increase is not 10% or more, no indexation is made. In the calculator you enter the index of the month before acquisition and of the month before sale (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 81; Council of State 3rd Chamber E.2024/2227, K.2025/64).

What is the capital gains exemption in 2026?

In 2026, 150,000 TL of the capital gains of a calendar year is exempt from income tax (2025: 120,000 TL; 2024: 87,000 TL; 2023: 55,000 TL). The exemption is annual; gains from several sales in the same year are added together (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 80; General Communiqué No. 332).

When is the capital gain declared and paid?

The gain is declared in the annual return from 1 to 25 March of the year following the sale; the tax is paid in two equal instalments in March and July (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 92, art. 117).

What if the price in the land register differs from the real price?

The cost and sale price are the real amounts; the tax office may not assess without examining the form and price of acquisition in the land registry records. The Council of State treated an assessment made without examining the registered price as an incomplete examination (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) duplicate art. 81; Council of State, Tax Chambers Board E.2022/858, K.2024/173).
