Inheritance Share and Reserved Portion Calculator under Turkish Law: Spouse, Children, Parents and Siblings

Go to the calculator

Shows, under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), who inherits under the statutory rules of the Turkish Civil Code (TMK) and each heir's statutory share as an exact fraction and a percentage: the group (zümre) system, representation at every degree, full and half siblings, the surviving spouse's share by group, and where the share of an heir who disclaims, is disinherited or is unworthy to inherit goes. It calculates each heir's reserved portion (forced heirship share) and the disposable portion; if you enter amounts, it shows the calculation estate and whether dispositions such as a will exceed the disposable portion. Each step is explained with its article number.

Short answer: Under Turkish law (Turkish Civil Code, TMK), the descendants inherit first (the children; in place of a child who died earlier, that child's own children); if there are no descendants, the mother and father and their descendants (siblings, nephews and nieces); failing them, the grandparents and their descendants. The surviving spouse takes 1/4 of the estate together with descendants, 1/2 together with the parents' group, 3/4 together with the grandparents and their children, and the whole estate if none of them exists. The reserved portion (forced heirship share) is half of the statutory share for descendants, one quarter for each parent, and for the spouse the whole statutory share together with descendants or the parents' group and three quarters otherwise; siblings have no reserved portion (TMK art. 495–499, art. 506).

Information tool; not legal advice.

How to use: enter the date of death and the surviving spouse's status and add the children by number; if there are no children, fill in the section on parents and siblings, and if there are none of them either, the section on grandparents, then press the calculate button; you can also start from one of the common family structures. To see the effect of a disclaimer, disinheritance or unworthiness on the shares, choose the relevant type of calculation with the buttons at the top of the form. The result appears on this page, just below the form. Results are preliminary estimates.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required fields: date of death and the surviving spouse's status; if no heir remains among the descendants, the status of the mother and father, and if no heir remains among them and the siblings either, the status of the four grandparents. A number left empty counts as 0. No name, identity number or file number is requested. Enter amounts in the Turkish number format (results are shown as 1,234.56): a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals (for example 1.200.000,50).

1. Date of death and surviving spouse

Heirship is determined by the provisions in force on the date of death. No calculation is made for deaths before 01.01.2002.

Surviving spouse required

A divorced spouse is not an heir. If a spouse dies while divorce proceedings are pending, one of the heirs continues the action and the surviving spouse's fault is proven, the spouse cannot inherit (TMK art. 181); in that case choose None.

2. Children and grandchildren (descendants)

Including adopted children and children born outside marriage whose parentage is established; children who died before the deceased are also counted. When you enter the number, a row opens for each child; with the "Parentage" option in the row you can mark an adopted child, a child born outside marriage or a stepchild, who is not an heir.

3. Parents and siblings (if there are no descendants)

Taken into account only if no heir remains among the descendants. The share of a deceased mother or father passes to that parent's descendants (the deceased's siblings, or nephews and nieces if the siblings have also died); a sibling with the same mother only or the same father only takes only from the common parent's share.

How the calculation is made

  1. First group, descendants: the children take equal shares; the place of a child who died before the deceased is taken by that child's own descendants at every degree (TMK art. 495). An adopted child and their descendants inherit from the adoptive parent like blood relatives (TMK art. 500); a child born outside marriage whose parentage is established inherits on the father's side like a child born in marriage (TMK art. 498).
  2. Second group, mother and father: if there are no descendants, the mother and father take equal shares; the place of a parent who died earlier is taken by that parent's descendants (the deceased's siblings, nephews and nieces); if there is no heir at all on one side, the whole estate goes to the other side (TMK art. 496). A sibling with the same mother only or the same father only takes only from the common parent's share.
  3. Third group, grandparents: if there is no second group either, the four grandparents take equal shares; the place of one who died earlier is taken by that grandparent's descendants; the share of one who died without descendants goes to the same side, and if one side is entirely empty, to the other side (TMK art. 497).
  4. Surviving spouse: one quarter of the estate together with descendants, one half together with the parents' group, three quarters together with the grandparents and their children, and the whole estate if none of them exists (TMK art. 499). Together with the spouse, only the grandparents and their children inherit in the third group (TMK art. 497/5). If there is no heir at all, the estate passes to the State (TMK art. 501).
  5. Disclaimer, disinheritance, unworthiness, waiver: the share of an heir who disclaims passes to those entitled as if the heir had not been alive (TMK art. 611/1); if all descendants disclaim, their shares pass to the surviving spouse, not to the grandchildren (TMK art. 613); if all the nearest statutory heirs disclaim, the estate is liquidated, the share does not pass to the descendants of those who disclaimed and the calculator gives no shares (TMK art. 612). The share of a disinherited or unworthy heir passes to that heir's descendants as if the heir had died earlier (TMK art. 511/2, art. 579/2). A person who waives the inheritance by agreement loses the status of heir; a waiver made for consideration also binds the descendants unless the agreement provides otherwise (TMK art. 528).
  6. Reserved portion: half of the statutory share for descendants and one quarter for each parent; for the surviving spouse, the whole statutory share together with descendants or the parents' group, three quarters otherwise (TMK art. 506). The siblings' reserved portion was abolished by Law No. 5650 (Official Gazette 10.05.2007). The part outside the reserved portions is the disposable portion; if there is no heir with a reserved portion, the deceased may dispose of the whole estate (TMK art. 505).
  7. Amounts: the disposable portion is calculated on the estate as at the date of death; debts, funeral expenses, the costs of sealing and inventorying and three months' maintenance of the persons who lived with and were supported by the deceased are deducted (TMK art. 507); lifetime gifts subject to abatement are added (TMK art. 508, art. 565). The statutory share amount is based on the net estate and the reserved portion amount on the calculation estate. Each amount is rounded down to the kuruş; the remaining kuruş are shown separately.

Not included in the calculation

  • Liquidation of the matrimonial property regime: the regime ends with death (TMK art. 225) and the spouse's claim from the regime (for example participation in the surplus value, TMK art. 236) is separated before the estate. Enter the value after liquidation as the estate amount.
  • Deaths before 01.01.2002: heirship is determined by the provisions in force on the date of death, that is, Law No. 743 (Law No. 4722 art. 17); the calculator gives no result. For deaths before 10.05.2007 the reserved portions are not shown if siblings are heirs.
  • Equalisation (TMK art. 669 ff.), the validity and interpretation of a will or an inheritance contract, and the order of abatement (TMK art. 570).
  • Allocation of the family home and household goods to the surviving spouse (TMK art. 652), substitute heirs (art mirasçılık), absence and cases with a foreign element.
  • The conditions, time limits and validity of a disclaimer, disinheritance, unworthiness or waiver; a waiver made in favour of a specific person (TMK art. 529/1) and abatement of payments made to a waiving heir (TMK art. 573): the calculator only shows the effect on the shares.
  • Simultaneous death (TMK art. 29/2), a child not yet born (TMK art. 582) and an heir whose survival is unknown (TMK art. 586) have no separate calculation; enter them as described in the notes of the result.
  • No personal data is requested; the calculation is made in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How large is the surviving spouse's share?

The spouse's share depends on the group (zümre) the spouse inherits with: one quarter of the estate together with descendants (children, grandchildren), one half together with the parents' group (mother, father, siblings and their children), three quarters together with the grandparents and their children, and the whole estate if none of them exists. Together with the spouse, the grandchildren of the grandparents (cousins) do not inherit. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 499, art. 497/5.

What is the reserved portion and who has one?

The reserved portion (forced heirship share) is the part the deceased cannot touch by dispositions upon death such as a will or an inheritance contract. It is half of the statutory share for descendants and one quarter for each parent; for the surviving spouse it is the whole statutory share when inheriting together with descendants or the parents' group, and three quarters otherwise. What remains outside the reserved portions is the disposable portion. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 505, art. 506.

Do siblings have a reserved portion?

No. Paragraph 3 of TMK art. 506, which gave siblings a reserved portion, was removed by Law No. 5650; the Law entered into force on its publication in the Official Gazette on 10.05.2007. For deaths after that date siblings can be statutory heirs but have no reserved portion. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 506; Law No. 5650 art. 2, art. 3.

Does an adopted child inherit?

Yes. The adopted child and their descendants inherit from the adoptive parent like blood relatives, and the adopted child's inheritance rights in their own family continue; in the calculator an adopted child is entered as a child. The adoptive parent and their relatives do not inherit from the adopted child. A child born outside marriage whose parentage was established by recognition or court judgment also inherits on the father's side like a child born in marriage. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 500, art. 498.

Who receives the share of an heir who disclaims the inheritance?

If a statutory heir disclaims the inheritance, their share passes to those entitled as if the heir had not been alive when the succession opened; the share of a disclaiming child therefore goes to that child's own descendants, or to the other heirs if there are none. If all descendants (for example all the children) disclaim, their shares pass to the surviving spouse, not to the grandchildren; if all the nearest statutory heirs disclaim, the estate is liquidated by the civil court of peace under the rules on bankruptcy. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 611, art. 612, art. 613.

How does the liquidation of the matrimonial property regime affect the inheritance share?

The matrimonial property regime ends with the death of a spouse and is liquidated first: under the statutory regime each spouse or their heirs are entitled to half of the surplus value belonging to the other spouse. The surviving spouse receives this claim before the estate is divided; only the value left to the deceased enters the estate, and the spouse also takes the inheritance share. The calculator does not liquidate the matrimonial property regime; when entering amounts, use the value after liquidation. Basis: Turkish Civil Code (Law No. 4721) art. 225, art. 236.
