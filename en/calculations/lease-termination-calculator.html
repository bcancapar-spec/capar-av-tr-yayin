Lease Termination Calculator under Turkish Law: Notice, End of the Lease and Time Limits for Eviction

Go to the calculator

For leases of dwellings, roofed business premises, other immovables, movables and usufructuary leases under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), calculates when a termination notice by the tenant, the landlord, a new owner or heirs must reach the other party at the latest, the day the lease ends and the last day for an eviction action or enforcement, under the Turkish Code of Obligations. Each date shows its article, the required form of notice (writing), mandatory mediation and the competent court; the result is shown as a timeline and can be downloaded as a PDF.

Short answer: Under Turkish law, in leases of dwellings and roofed business premises the tenant ends a fixed-term lease by written notice given at least fifteen days before the term expires; otherwise the lease is extended for one year. The landlord cannot rely on expiry of the term: after a ten-year extension period it may terminate by notice at least three months before the end of each extension year, or for the grounds listed in the law (own need, reconstruction, need of a new owner, written undertaking to vacate, two justified warnings, default) mostly by filing an action within one month (Turkish Code of Obligations art. 347–353).

How the calculation works

  1. Tenant's notice: in a fixed-term lease of a dwelling or roofed business premises, without notice at least fifteen days before the term expires the lease is extended on the same terms for one year (Turkish Code of Obligations art. 347(1)). Notice must be in writing (art. 348).
  2. Landlord's ordinary termination: the landlord cannot rely on the expiry of a fixed term; after the ten-year extension period it may terminate without reasons by notice at least three months before the end of each extension year. In an indefinite lease the landlord may terminate under the general rules once ten years have passed since the start (art. 347(2)).
  3. Termination periods: in an indefinite lease of immovable property, without local custom, notice of three months is required for the end of a six-month period counted from the start; notice that misses the period is valid for the next one (art. 328, 329). Movables: three days (art. 330(1)); a movable from a professional lessor: the tenant, one month before the end of a three-month period (art. 330(2)); usufructuary leases: six months (art. 367, 368).
  4. Eviction actions: for own need and reconstruction (art. 350) and another dwelling (art. 352(3)) from the end of the term, for two justified warnings from the end of the lease year in which they were given (art. 352(2)), and for an undertaking from the date undertaken (art. 352(1)), the action is filed within one month. A new owner who notifies in writing within one month from acquisition may sue after six months, or within one month from the end of the lease (art. 351). If the landlord notifies within the time limit in writing that it will sue, the time is extended by one lease year (art. 353).
  5. Default and duty of care: the tenant is given in writing at least thirty days for dwellings and roofed business premises and at least ten days for other leases (sixty days in usufructuary leases), running from the day after notification (art. 315, 362). For breach of the duty of care and respect for neighbours, at least thirty days are given for dwellings and roofed business premises (art. 316).
  6. Counting: a period in days is counted without the first day; a period in months or years ends on the corresponding day of the last month, or the last day of that month if there is none (art. 92). A last day falling on a holiday moves to the next working day (art. 93; for actions, Code of Civil Procedure art. 93). Official holidays follow Law No. 2429; eves of religious holidays are half days and are not treated as holidays.
  7. Mediation and jurisdiction: except for eviction through enforcement without judgment, applying to a mediator before suing is a condition of the action in lease disputes (Law No. 6325 art. 18/B; 01.09.2023). The civil court of peace has jurisdiction (Code of Civil Procedure art. 4(1)(a)).

What is not calculated

  • "Reasonable" or "appropriate" times for which the law gives no number of days: bankruptcy of the tenant (art. 332), defects (art. 304–306), early return of the property (art. 325). No date is produced for these grounds.
  • The last day for an eviction action for default or breach of the duty of care (the law sets no time limit).
  • Fact-dependent assessments such as the validity of service, the content of a warning or whether a need is genuine.
  • Rent, rent increases and amounts of compensation.

What this tool does not calculate

  • Sublease and transfer of the lease: For dwellings and roofed business premises, subletting and transfer of use require the landlord's written consent (TBK art. 322); transfer of the lease also requires written consent, which for business premises the landlord may not refuse without just cause (TBK art. 323).
  • Return of the deposit: For dwellings and roofed business premises the deposit may not exceed three months' rent; money is placed in a time deposit and securities with a bank. If within three months after the lease ends the landlord does not notify the bank of an action or enforcement, the bank returns the deposit to the tenant (TBK art. 342). This period is not calculated.
  • Determination and increase of rent: Rent for a new period and the increase limit are not termination time limits.
  • Condominium disputes: Disputes under the Condominium Law (Law No. 634) are not lease termination.
  • Public leases and compensation for unlawful occupation: Special legislation applies to leases by public bodies and to compensation for unlawful occupation (ecrimisil).
  • Short-term tourist rentals: Letting dwellings to tourists is separately regulated with a permit and administrative sanctions.
  • Financial leasing: Financial leasing is governed by Law No. 6361, not by the lease rules of the TBK.

A tenant who wants to leave early

In a fixed-term lease, a tenant who leaves before the end of the term remains liable for rent for a reasonable time in which the property could be re-let on similar terms. The obligation ends if the tenant finds a solvent new tenant ready to take over the lease on the same terms whom the landlord could be expected to accept; the landlord must also deduct the costs saved and the benefits obtained from other use (TBK art. 325). The reasonable time is set by the judge on the facts, so the calculator gives no date. Give written notice before leaving, propose the candidate in writing and hand over the keys against a record. If there is good cause, the "Good cause" route (TBK art. 331) is calculated separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a tenant terminate a lease in Türkiye?

In a fixed-term lease of a dwelling or roofed business premises the tenant gives the landlord written notice at least fifteen days before the term expires; without notice the lease is extended on the same terms for one year (Turkish Code of Obligations, Law No. 6098, art. 347/1, 348). In an indefinite lease the tenant may terminate at any time for the end of a six-month period counted from the start of the lease, with three months' notice (Law No. 6098 art. 347/2, 328, 329). Notice takes effect on the day it reaches the other party.

Can the landlord evict the tenant before ten years have passed?

In a fixed-term lease of a dwelling or roofed business premises the landlord cannot end the lease merely because the term has expired. Termination without giving reasons is possible only after the ten-year extension period added to the term, by written notice at least three months before the end of each extension year (Law No. 6098 art. 347/1). Before that, the lease can be ended only on the statutory grounds (own need, reconstruction, new owner, undertaking to vacate, two justified warnings, default, breach of the duty of care).

When must an eviction action for own need be filed?

In a fixed-term lease, within one month from the end of the term or of the extended lease year (Law No. 6098 art. 350). If the landlord notifies the tenant in writing within that period that it will sue, the time to sue is extended by one lease year (Law No. 6098 art. 353). In an indefinite lease the period starts on the date set by the six-month termination periods and three months' notice. Applying to a mediator before suing is a condition of the action (Law No. 6325 art. 18/B).

When can a person who has bought the property evict the tenant?

If the new owner notifies the tenant in writing of its need within one month from acquisition, it may sue six months after acquisition; alternatively it may sue within one month from the end of the lease term (Law No. 6098 art. 351). The Court of Cassation holds that the one-month notice cannot be cured later and that the action after six months may be brought until the end of the lease (3rd Civil Chamber, 09.09.2025, 2025/2624 E., 2025/3931 K.).

What can the landlord do if the rent is not paid?

The landlord may give the tenant a written time limit and state that the lease will be terminated if payment is not made within it. The limit is at least thirty days for dwellings and roofed business premises and at least ten days for other leases, running from the day after notification (Law No. 6098 art. 315); in usufructuary leases it is sixty days (Law No. 6098 art. 362). Enforcement with a payment order containing the warning is also possible; if no objection is made and the rent is not paid within the warning period, eviction may be requested from the enforcement court within six months after that period ends (Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law art. 269, 269/a).

When can enforcement be started on an undertaking to vacate?

If, after delivery of the premises, the tenant undertook in writing to vacate on a specific date and did not, the landlord may apply to the enforcement office or sue within one month from that date (Law No. 6098 art. 352/1; Law No. 2004 art. 272). Mediation is not required for eviction through enforcement without judgment; it is required for an action.

Is mediation mandatory in lease disputes?

Yes. Since 1 September 2023, for disputes arising from a lease other than eviction through enforcement without judgment, applying to a mediator before suing is a condition of the action (Law No. 6325 art. 18/B; Law No. 7445 art. 43). Time limits for forfeiture do not run from the application to the mediation office until the final report (Law No. 6325 art. 18/A).

Which court has jurisdiction?

All disputes arising from a lease, including eviction and rent claims, are heard by the civil court of peace regardless of value, except eviction through enforcement without judgment, which belongs to the enforcement office and enforcement court (Law No. 6100 art. 4/1-a).

What happens if the last day falls on a holiday?

A performance date or last day falling on a statutory holiday moves to the next working day (Law No. 6098 art. 93); a last day to sue falling on an official holiday ends on the next working day (Law No. 6100 art. 93). The last day for notice is not moved: the notice must reach the other party before it.

Does the rent increase cap affect termination?

The 25 per cent cap on rent increases for dwellings was a temporary rule introduced by Law No. 7409 in 2022 and extended by Law No. 7456 to lease periods renewed up to 01.07.2024 (Law No. 6098 provisional art. 1 and 2). For periods renewed after 2 July 2024, the increase cannot exceed the twelve-month average change in the consumer price index (Law No. 6098 art. 344). These rules concern rent; they do not change termination or eviction time limits.

Legislation

  • Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 (TBK): all termination and eviction routes, counting of periods and holidays (art. 92, 93, 304–306, 315, 316, 325, 327–333, 339, 347–355, 362, 367–369)
  • Law No. 6325 on Mediation in Civil Disputes: mandatory mediation and forfeiture periods not running during it (art. 16, 18/A, 18/B)
  • Code of Civil Procedure No. 6100 (HMK): competent court and a last day to sue falling on a holiday (art. 4, 93, 103)
  • Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law No. 2004 (İİK): eviction through enforcement: payment order with warning and undertaking to vacate (art. 269, 269/a, 272)
  • Law No. 6101 on the Entry into Force of the TBK: deferral of termination after the ten-year extension for leases made before 01.07.2012 (provisional art. 2)
  • Law No. 6217: some TBK provisions not applied to merchant tenants until 01.07.2020 (provisional art. 2)
