Loss of Support Compensation Calculator: Spouse, Children and Parents after a Fatal Traffic or Work Accident in Türkiye

Go to the calculator

Estimates the loss of support compensation (destekten yoksun kalma tazminatı) of the spouse, children, mother and father of a person who died, under Turkish law, with the actuarial method used by the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay): TRH 2010 life table, known and unknown periods, active and passive periods, the share system, support periods of children, the spouse's probability of remarriage, the assumed family and upbringing costs when a child dies, the deceased's fault, the SGK set-off and the death cover of compulsory traffic insurance. Each step shows the statutory provision and the decision it relies on.

Short answer: Loss of support compensation (destekten yoksun kalma tazminatı) under Turkish law is the monetary value of the support that the spouse, children, mother and father lose when the person who maintained them dies (Turkish Code of Obligations (TBK) art. 53/3). In the practice of the Court of Cassation (Yargıtay) the deceased's income is shared by units (2 each for the deceased and the spouse, 1 each for every child, the mother and the father); the period follows the TRH 2010 life table, children are supported until 18 (boys), 22 (girls) or 25 (higher education), and the deceased's fault, the spouse's probability of remarriage and SGK payments that can be recovered are deducted.

Information tool; not legal advice. Preliminary estimate; it does not replace an actuarial report.

How to use: enter the details of the deceased, add the persons entitled and press the calculate button; the result appears right below the form with a tabular report for each person and a PDF.

What you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required: type of event, the deceased's sex, dates of birth, death and calculation, employment status, marital status and at least one person entitled. Optional fields left empty are not taken into account. No names, file numbers or court names are asked.

How the calculation works

  1. Period of support: the probable end of life of the deceased follows the TRH 2010 life table for the age on the date of death; each person is supported until that date or until leaving support, whichever is earlier (Yargıtay Assembly of Civil Chambers E.2024/643 K.2025/264). For traffic and other events before 01/06/2015, where the decisions do not settle the table, a second amount with the PMF 1931 table is shown (4th CC E.2023/2759 K.2024/3241: PMF 1931 for an event of 20/01/2012).
  2. Income: the net wage of a working deceased is related to the minimum wage; the active period ends at 60 in traffic and other events (65 for a deceased over 60 who was actually working; 4th CC E.2022/6645 K.2023/1126), and at 60 for men and 55 for women in work accidents; afterwards the net minimum wage is used. For a homemaker, student or retired deceased the net minimum wage without AGİ is used for all periods (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2024/14583 K.2026/4782; E.2022/12664 K.2025/7307).
  3. Periods: the known period from the date of death to the calculation date uses the minimum wage periods; afterwards, the unknown period uses the minimum wage on the calculation date with equal increase and discount (progressive annuity, no technical interest) (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2025/10818 K.2025/16778).
  4. Shares: 2 units each for the deceased and the spouse, 1 unit each for every child, the mother and the father; without children 50% each for the deceased and the spouse. A child's share passes to the deceased, the spouse and the other children; one parent's share passes to the other (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2022/12664 K.2025/7307).
  5. Children and spouse: children are supported until 18 (boys), 22 (girls) or 25 (higher education); a remarried spouse until remarriage, otherwise the AYİM probability of remarriage (5 points lower for each child under 18) is deducted from the spouse's future-period compensation (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2024/6175 K.2025/15132; Assembly of Civil Chambers E.2017/2038 K.2019/979). If no rate is entered, the table rate is applied to a female spouse; as the table for women cannot be applied to a male spouse, you enter his rate (Yargıtay 17th CC E.2017/3946 K.2017/10827).
  6. Death of a child: income from 18, marriage after military service and two children are assumed; the parents' shares are 25%, 16%, 14% and 12.5%; upbringing costs of 5% of the minimum wage are deducted from each parent until 18 (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2024/2701 K.2024/4307; E.2023/3240 K.2026/1743).
  7. Reductions and set-offs: after upbringing costs, the deceased's fault, gratuitous carriage if selected (20%), the amount SGK may recover and payments are deducted in turn; each person's deductions come only from that person's compensation (Turkish Code of Obligations art. 51, 52, 55; Law No. 5510 art. 21; Yargıtay 10th CC E.2024/16205 K.2025/11852).
  8. Traffic insurance: the insurer is liable up to the death cover per person on the date of the accident; the cover is single for all persons entitled and shared pro rata if exceeded (Highway Traffic Law art. 85, 91; Yargıtay 4th CC E.2023/2759 K.2024/3241).

Not included

  • Non-pecuniary damages (Turkish Code of Obligations art. 56/2; outside traffic insurance, Highway Traffic Law art. 92/f).
  • Funeral costs (art. 53/1): a separate item limited to documented and necessary costs.
  • Claims of heirs who were not supported; other relatives such as siblings must prove support separately.
  • Interest, several deaths and the cover per accident, recourse of SGK against the insurer, updating of the insurer's payment (you enter the current value).
  • Where the deceased was unable to work for some time before death, the bodily injury (incapacity) for that period is calculated separately: Bodily Injury Compensation Calculator.
  • Limitation: you can check the period with the Limitation Period Calculator.
  • No personal data is asked; the calculation runs in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Who can claim loss of support compensation?

Persons whom the deceased actually and regularly maintained, or would have maintained in the future: as a rule the spouse, the children, the mother and the father (Turkish Code of Obligations (TBK) art. 53/3). Being an heir is not enough; a support relationship is required, and other relatives such as siblings may claim if they prove it. The calculator covers the spouse, children, mother and father. Basis: Law No. 6098 (TBK) art. 53/3.

How is the deceased's income shared among the persons entitled?

Under the settled share system of the 4th Civil Chamber of the Court of Cassation the deceased and the spouse take 2 units each, every child, the mother and the father 1 unit each; without children the deceased and the spouse take 50% each. When a child leaves support its share passes to the deceased, the spouse and the other children, not to the parents; when the mother or the father leaves, the share passes to the other parent. A share is set aside even for a living person who makes no claim (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2022/12664 K.2025/7307). Basis: Law No. 6098 (TBK) art. 53/3 and 55/1.

Until what age are children supported?

Until 25 if they attend or will attend higher education; otherwise until 18 for boys and 22 for girls (Yargıtay 4th CC E.2024/6175 K.2025/15132). Basis: Law No. 6098 (TBK) art. 53/3.

How does the spouse's probability of remarriage affect the compensation?

If the spouse has remarried, support is calculated until the date of remarriage. Otherwise the rate in the remarriage table of the Military High Administrative Court (AYİM) is taken for the spouse's age on the calculation (report) date; it is reduced by 5 points for each child under 18 and deducted from the spouse's compensation for the future period (Yargıtay Assembly of Civil Chambers E.2017/2038 K.2019/979). If the field is left empty, the calculator applies the table rate to a female spouse; as the table was taken from open sources rather than an AYİM publication, you can enter a different rate if you know one. The table for women cannot be applied to a male spouse, so you enter his rate (Yargıtay 17th CC E.2017/3946 K.2017/10827). Basis: Law No. 6098 (TBK) art. 53/3 and 55/1.

Can the parents claim when a child dies?

Yes. The Court of Cassation assumes that a young child will start earning at 18, set aside half of the income for the parents until marriage (25% each), 16% each after marriage, 14% with one child and 12.5% with two children; upbringing costs of 5% of the minimum wage are deducted from each parent until the child would have turned 18 (parents' shares: Yargıtay 4th CC E.2024/2701 K.2024/4307; age 18 and 5%: E.2023/8480 K.2024/7836; from each parent and before fault: E.2023/3240 K.2026/1743). Basis: Law No. 6098 (TBK) art. 53/3.

How much does the traffic insurer pay at most?

Up to the death cover per person in force on the date of the accident; this amount is per deceased and single for all persons entitled. If their total exceeds the cover, it is shared pro rata; the operator and the driver are liable for the excess (Highway Traffic Law art. 85, 91; Yargıtay 4th CC E.2023/2759 K.2024/3241). Basis: Law No. 2918 (KTK) art. 85 and 91.
