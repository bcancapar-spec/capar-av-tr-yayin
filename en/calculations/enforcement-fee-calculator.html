Enforcement Fee Calculator under Turkish Law (2026): Filing Fee, Advance Fee, Collection Fee and Prison Construction Fee

Go to the calculator

Calculates, under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), the fees payable in enforcement proceedings item by item, using Tariff (1) of Fees Law No. 492, the Fees Law General Communiqué (Serial No. 98) that sets the fixed amounts for 2026 and Law No. 2548: the filing fee, the advance fee of 5 per thousand in proceedings without a judgment and the power of attorney fee when proceedings are started; the collection fee by stage of payment or collection, the set-off of the advance fee and the prison construction fee; the collection fee for eviction and delivery of movables. All stages are compared in one table; every line shows its tariff item.

Short answer: Under Turkish law, when enforcement proceedings without a judgment are started in 2026, a filing fee of TRY 732 and an advance fee of 5 per thousand of the claim are paid (Fees Law No. 492 art. 29; Tariff (1) B-I-1; Communiqué Serial No. 98). The collection fee is 4.55% if the debt is paid after service of the payment order and before attachment, 9.10% after attachment and before sale and 11.38% if collected through a sale; the advance fee is set off against it. A prison construction fee of 2% of the amount collected is also charged (Law No. 2548 art. 1).

Information tool; not legal advice. Tariff year: 2026 (Harçlar Kanunu Genel Tebliği (Seri No: 98), Official Gazette 31 December 2025, issue 33124 (5. Mükerrer)).

How to use: choose the date and type of the proceedings, enter the amount claimed (principal, accrued interest and costs), choose the stage for which you want to see the collection fee and press the calculate button. The result appears on this page, just below the form. Results are preliminary estimates; the enforcement office cashier's calculation prevails.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required fields: date and type of the proceedings; the amount for money claims and delivery of movables, the annual rent for eviction. Enter amounts in the Turkish number format (results are shown as 1,234.56): a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals (for example 1.234.567,89). No name, identity number or file number is requested.

1. Proceedings

Only the 2026 tariff is loaded; no amount is given for other years.

An advance fee of 5 per thousand is charged in proceedings without a judgment, on negotiable instruments and on pledges (Fees Law art. 29).

2. Amounts and stage

The total of principal, accrued interest and costs in the enforcement request; for delivery of movables, the value of the movable (art. 19).

In eviction the collection fee is calculated on one year's rent (Fees Law art. 18).

Optional: if left empty, the full amount claimed is assumed to be collected. Interest accruing after the start of the proceedings is not included in the fee base (Fees Law art. 20).

If ticked, collection fees apply at one quarter (Tariff (1) B-I-3-h).

3. Lawyer and steps outside the office
Are the proceedings started by a lawyer?

The bar stamp is not in the Fees Law but in art. 27 of the Attorneys' Act; enter the current amount if you know it.

If you would like to assess the result together with your own proceedings, see the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law page or the Contact page.

How the calculation works

  1. Filing fee: TRY 732 in every proceeding in 2026 (Tariff (1) B-I-1; Communiqué Serial No. 98). For enforcing judgments of indefinite value, a further TRY 732 fee for carrying out enforcement is charged (B-I-2).
  2. Advance fee: in enforcement requests not based on a judgment, 5 per thousand of the claim is charged in advance and set off against the fee charged at the end (Fees Law art. 29). There is no such advance fee in enforcement based on a judgment.
  3. Collection fee: 4.55% of amounts paid after service of the payment or enforcement order and before attachment, 9.10% of amounts paid after attachment and before sale, 11.38% of amounts collected through sale, 4.55% of amounts collected through attachment of salaries and wages (B-I-3-a–d). The fee becomes due when enforcement is carried out and is paid when the claim is paid or, failing payment, within 15 days (art. 28/b).
  4. Prison construction fee: 2% of the money collected on claims of a definite amount, with or without a judgment; half the collection fee when judgments of indefinite value are enforced. This fee cannot be charged to the debtor (Law No. 2548 art. 1).
  5. Eviction and delivery: the collection fee is calculated on one year's rent for immovables and on the value shown in the judgment or enforcement request for movables (art. 18, art. 19); 2.27% if the premises are vacated or the movable delivered upon service of the enforcement order, 4.55% if this is done by the enforcement office (B-I-3-f, g).
  6. Other items: TRY 1,718.80 for each attachment, delivery or sale step carried out outside the office by an officer (B-III); TRY 104 for a copy of the power of attorney certified by the lawyer (D-I-c). Enforcement costs are borne by the debtor; the creditor advances them (Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law art. 59).
  7. Rounding: no rule on rounding the proportional fee to the kuruş was found in the Fees Law or the communiqué; each item is rounded to the kuruş (half a kuruş rounded up). The enforcement office cashier's calculation prevails.

What is not included

  • Notification and postal costs, custody and storage costs, the administration fee (B-I-4).
  • The Central Enforcement System fee for subscription claims (art. 29/A), bankruptcy and concordat fees.
  • The one-quarter rate for housing finance claims and pledged claims of the Housing Development Administration (B-I-3-h), separate fees on partial payments, the half fee on withdrawal (art. 23): the calculator only shows them as notes.
  • Years other than 2026: no amount is given for a year without a loaded tariff.
  • No personal data is requested; the calculation is made in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advance fee in enforcement proceedings?

In enforcement requests not based on a judgment (general attachment, negotiable instruments, realisation of a pledge), 5 per thousand of the claim is paid in advance when the proceedings are started and is set off against the fee charged at the end (Fees Law No. 492 art. 29). If the creditor has to go to court, the advance fee is refunded or, on request, set off against the court fees. There is no such advance fee in enforcement based on a judgment.

What are the collection fee rates?

Under Tariff (1) B-I-3, the collection fee is 4.55% of amounts paid after service of the payment or enforcement order and before attachment, 9.10% of amounts paid after attachment and before sale, 11.38% of amounts collected by selling attached or pledged assets and 4.55% of amounts collected through attachment of salaries and wages. The fee becomes due when enforcement is carried out (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/b).

Who pays the collection fee?

The debtor is liable for the collection fee (Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law art. 15: fees and costs are borne by the debtor). The fee is deducted from the money paid in to the enforcement office; the creditor continues enforcement for the amount deducted and recovers it from the debtor (Court of Cassation 12th Civil Chamber 2019/5441 E., 2020/2900 K.). The collection fee is paid when the claim is paid or, if no payment is made, within 15 days after the fee becomes due (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/b). If the creditor withdraws, half of the collection fee on the withdrawn amount is charged, or the full fee if the attached asset has already been sold (art. 23).

What is the prison construction fee?

Under Law No. 2548 art. 1, a fee of 2% of the money collected by enforcement offices on claims of a definite amount, with or without a judgment, and of half the collection fee for enforcing judgments of indefinite value is charged. Under the law this fee cannot be charged to the debtor.

How is the fee calculated in eviction proceedings?

In proceedings for the eviction and delivery of immovable property the collection fee is calculated on one year's rent (Fees Law No. 492 art. 18). Under Tariff (1) B-I-3-f the rate is 2.27% if the premises are vacated upon service of the enforcement order and 4.55% if eviction is carried out by the enforcement office. For delivery of movables, the value is the one shown in the judgment or the enforcement request (art. 19).

Which proceedings are exempt from fees?

Proceedings with a value of up to TRY 50 (except those based on commercial papers) and proceedings for maintenance of up to TRY 100 a month are exempt (Fees Law No. 492 art. 13). When claims awarded to workers by labour courts are enforced, no fee is charged to the worker; if the worker's claim is upheld, the fee is collected from the debtor at the end (art. 33). Legal aid also provides temporary exemption from enforcement costs (Code of Civil Procedure art. 335).
