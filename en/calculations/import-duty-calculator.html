Import Duty Calculator for Türkiye: Customs Value, Customs Duty, ÖTV, VAT and Country Regimes

Go to the calculator

Choose what you are doing (import, export, parcel, traveller) and the partner country, then enter the values and the rates from the official tariff query: under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye) the calculator shows the customs value, the base and amount of each tax, the total tax burden and the duty-paid cost line by line with the legal basis; the country panel shows the trade regime with Türkiye, the document needed for a preferential rate and the official tariff source of the other country (Customs Law No. 4458 art. 24–30; VAT Law No. 3065 art. 21; ÖTV Law No. 4760 art. 11).

Short answer: Under Turkish law import taxes are calculated as a chain: first the customs value (invoice price + freight and insurance up to the place of entry into Türkiye + additions such as royalties); customs duty and additional customs duty are levied on that value; the special consumption tax (ÖTV) base adds those duties, and the VAT base further adds ÖTV and untaxed costs incurred until the declaration is registered (Customs Law No. 4458 art. 24, 27, 28; VAT Law No. 3065 art. 21; ÖTV Law No. 4760 art. 11). Rates depend on the 12-digit tariff code and origin; look them up in the official tariff query.

Information tool; not legal advice. Not binding tariff information either: you enter the rates, and if you enter a wrong rate or code, the result is wrong.

How to use: choose what you are doing, then the region and country; enter values and costs; enter the tax rates from the official tariff query (choose the specific, mixed or quota method where needed) and press the calculate button. The result appears on this page, just below the form.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

How the calculation works

  1. Customs value: freight, insurance, loading and handling up to the place of entry into Türkiye, commissions other than buying commissions, packing, assists and royalties related to the goods and a condition of sale are added to the price (Law No. 4458 art. 27); transport and insurance after entry, installation, financing interest, reproduction rights and buying commissions are deducted if shown separately (art. 28). The law sets no default insurance amount.
  2. Exchange rate: the Central Bank selling rate in force when the customs debt arises (registration of the declaration) (art. 30; Customs Regulation art. 57).
  3. Customs duty and additional customs duty: by tariff code and origin, on value, per unit (specific), both (mixed) or at in-quota and out-of-quota rates. Non-EU-origin goods arriving with an A.TR bear additional customs duty at the 'Other Countries' rate (Decision No. 3351 art. 1/2): origin, not the country of shipment, decides.
  4. Anti-dumping duty, additional financial liability, KKDF, stamp duty: measures are product- and origin-specific; KKDF applies only to acceptance credit, deferred letters of credit and open account, on the deferred invoice amount, and enters the VAT and ÖTV base (Decision No. 88/12944 art. 3; Law No. 3065 art. 21/b; Council of State 7th Chamber 2025/1836, 2025/3948 and 2018/595, 2021/4177); the customs declaration bears a fixed stamp duty, TRY 1,605.80 in 2026 (Law No. 488 table (1) IV-2-c; Stamp Duty Communiqué No. 71), which as a tax paid on import enters the VAT and ÖTV base (Law No. 3065 art. 21/b).
  5. ÖTV: for goods in lists (II), (III)(A) and (IV) the base consists of the VAT base elements excluding ÖTV (Law No. 4760 art. 11/3); pre-registration storage costs enter the ÖTV base even if invoiced with VAT (Council of State 7th Chamber 2024/2632, 2025/4351).
  6. VAT: the base is the customs value plus all taxes, duties, fees and levies paid on import and untaxed costs incurred until registration (Law No. 3065 art. 21); the date of the service, not the invoice, counts, and only the part of demurrage up to registration is included (Council of State Tax Chambers Board 2022/542, 2022/1423; 7th Chamber 2021/5000, 2023/4960).
  7. Rounding: each tax line is rounded to the kuruş (half up); the customs system may differ by a few kuruş.

Where to find the rates

  1. Open the Tariff Search application referred to in the Ministry of Trade notice Gümrük Vergileri Sorgulama (E-İşlemler › Tarife Arama).
  2. Enter the 12-digit code and check that the description matches your product. Classification is the declarant's responsibility; binding tariff information may be requested.
  3. In the customs duty line read the group of the country of origin (EU, the free trade partner, or third countries / Other Countries); the country panel tells you which group to use.
  4. Read additional customs duty, anti-dumping duty, additional financial liability, tariff quota and surveillance lines by origin. If an amount is per unit (e.g. USD/kg) choose "Specific"; if a percentage and a unit amount are combined choose "Mixed".
  5. Take ÖTV and VAT rates from the same query or the Revenue Administration lists; confirm the KKDF rate with your bank.
  6. Rates may change; use the declaration date. If you enter a wrong rate, the result is wrong.

Non-tax obligations and documents

  • A.TR and EUR.1 movement certificates and certificates of origin are issued by chambers of commerce and industry under their annual fee schedule (Law No. 5174 art. 26/1-o, 71/1-b).
  • Exporters must join the relevant exporters' association, which collects up to 1 per mille of FOB and an additional levy of up to 3 per ten thousand (Law No. 5910 art. 4, 18/3, 18/6).
  • For goods under surveillance, a unit value below the surveillance value requires a surveillance document; the surveillance value is not the customs value (Import Surveillance Communiqué art. 2, 7/2).
  • Product safety inspections (such as TAREKS) and export restrictions are non-tax obligations; check them for your product.

Outside the scope

  • Tariff classification and binding tariff information: the calculator checks only the format of the code.
  • Customs penalties (Law No. 4458 art. 234–241) and smuggling; see the article on additional customs assessment and penalty decisions.
  • Inward processing, customs warehousing, temporary admission and transit (including TIR at land borders).
  • Product-level determination of surveillance, safeguard and anti-dumping measures: use the official tariff query.
  • Foreign exchange obligations such as repatriation of export proceeds; sanctions and export controls of the other country and third countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GTİP code?

The GTİP is the 12-digit code of the goods in the Turkish Customs Tariff Schedule. The first 6 digits match the international Harmonized System; the remaining digits are national. Duty is then levied on the customs value of the goods (Law No. 4458 art. 24). The calculator checks the format and shows the code in the report; it does not classify the goods.

Which costs are included in the customs value?

Freight, insurance, loading and handling up to the place of entry into Türkiye, commissions other than buying commissions, packing, assists and royalties that relate to the goods and are a condition of sale are added to the price (Law No. 4458 art. 27). Transport after entry, installation, financing interest, reproduction rights and buying commissions are excluded if shown separately (art. 28).

Which exchange rate is used?

Foreign currency amounts are converted at the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye selling rate in force on the date the customs debt arises, i.e. when the declaration is registered (Law No. 4458 art. 30; Customs Regulation art. 57). You enter this rate yourself.

How much tax is due on parcels ordered from abroad?

Since 6 February 2026 the flat-rate tax on postal and express parcels sent to individuals applies only to prescribed medicines and food supplements up to EUR 1,500: 30% if sent directly from the EU, 60% from other countries, plus 20% for goods in ÖTV list IV (Decision No. 2009/15481 art. 62/1, as amended by Presidential Decision No. 10813). Books for personal use are exempt up to EUR 150 and taxed at 0% up to EUR 1,500. Other goods follow the general import rules.

What applies on exports from Türkiye?

Export deliveries are exempt from VAT (Law No. 3065 art. 11/1-a) and export documents, including customs declarations, are exempt from stamp duty (Law No. 488 additional art. 2). Exporters must be members of the relevant exporters' association, which collects up to 1 per mille of the FOB value plus an additional levy of up to 3 per ten thousand (Law No. 5910 art. 4, 18).
