Inheritance Tax Calculator for Türkiye: Exemptions, Tax by Bracket, Filing Deadline and Instalments

Go to the calculator

Enter the date of death or gift, your relationship and the value of the assets in your share: under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye) the calculator deducts debts and the exemption, calculates the tax bracket by bracket and shows the filing deadline and the six instalments over three years (Law No. 7338 art. 4, 9, 12, 16, 19).

Short answer: How it works under Turkish law: each heir's share is taxed separately: debts are deducted from the value of the share, the exemption for children, grandchildren and the spouse (2026: 2,907,136 TL; for a spouse without descendants 5,817,845 TL) is deducted, and the rest is taxed at progressive rates from 1% to 10%. Gifts have an exemption of 66,935 TL and rates of 10% to 30%; gifts from a parent, spouse or child are taxed at half these rates. The tax is paid in six instalments over three years, in May and November (Law No. 7338 art. 4, 16, 19).

Information tool; not legal advice. It does not replace a tax return, an accountant's work or legal advice: if you enter a wrong value, the result is wrong.

If you do not know your inheritance share, first find it with the Inheritance Share Calculator; this calculator only calculates the tax.

How the calculation works

  1. Share: the tax applies to each heir's share, not the whole estate; find your share with the Inheritance Share Calculator and enter the value of the assets in that share.
  2. Debts: the deceased's documented debts, tax debts and funeral costs are deducted from the value of the share (art. 12).
  3. Exemption: an exemption in each share of a child, grandchild, adopted child and spouse; a higher one for a spouse without descendants; a separate, lower one for gifts (art. 4).
  4. Rates: the remaining base is taxed bracket by bracket; gift rates are higher, and half of them apply to gifts from a parent, spouse or child (art. 16).
  5. Filing and payment: the return within four, six or eight months (one month for gifts); the tax in six instalments over three years, each May and November (art. 9, art. 19).

Frequently asked questions

Whose share is the Turkish inheritance tax calculated on?

Each heir is a separate taxpayer for the assets passing to them; the tax applies to the heir's share, not to the whole estate. The Council of State (7th Chamber) held that only the heir's own share of money in a joint account passes to that heir (E.2019/3648, K.2022/4322). You can find your share with the Inheritance Share Calculator (Law No. 7338 art. 4/b).

What is the inheritance tax exemption in Türkiye in 2026?

For deaths in 2026, 2,907,136 TL of the share of each child, grandchild, adopted child and the spouse is exempt; where the deceased has no descendants, the spouse's exemption is 5,817,845 TL. Parents and siblings have no separate exemption (Law No. 7338 art. 4/b; General Communiqué No. 57).

How is gift tax calculated in Türkiye?

For gifts and other gratuitous acquisitions in 2026 the first 66,935 TL is exempt and the rest is taxed at rates rising from 10% to 30%. Gifts from a parent, spouse or child are taxed at half these rates; a gift from an adopted child to the adoptive parent does not get this reduction (Law No. 7338 art. 4/d, art. 16).

When is the inheritance tax return due?

If the death occurred in Türkiye and the heir is in Türkiye, the return is due within four months of the death; six months if the heir is abroad; four, six or eight months if the death occurred abroad; for gifts, within one month of acquisition. The calculator shows the last day on the calendar and does not shift it for holidays (Law No. 7338 art. 9).

Are the estate's debts and funeral costs deducted?

Yes. The deceased's documented debts and tax debts and the funeral costs are deducted from the value of the assets if shown in the return; the exemption applies to the remaining value. Under the participation in acquired property regime, the surviving spouse's participation claim is separated from the estate first (Law No. 7338 art. 12; Council of State 7th Chamber E.2022/3621, K.2025/1923).

Which year's amounts apply?

For inheritance the amounts of the year of death apply, for gifts those of the year the asset is legally acquired. For claims arising later, such as compensation for cancellation of a title deed, the Council of State (7th Chamber) took the date the money was paid (E.2022/593, K.2024/4885). The calculator holds the amounts for 2023–2026 (Law No. 7338 art. 16).
