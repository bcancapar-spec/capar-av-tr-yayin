Court Fee Calculator under Turkish Law (2026): Filing Fee, Advance Fee, Amendment Fee and Appeal Fees

Go to the calculator

Calculates, under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), the court fees payable to the court cashier when a case is filed, item by item, using Tariff (1) of Fees Law No. 492 and the Fees Law General Communiqué (Serial No. 98) that sets the fixed amounts for 2026: the filing fee, the advance judgment and writ fee in a proportional case (one quarter; one twentieth in claims for death or bodily injury), the fixed judgment fee, the power of attorney fee and the advance on costs; it also shows the balance of the judgment fee payable after service of the judgment. For an amendment or increase of the claim it gives the advance fee on the increased value, and for an appeal the appeal fee. Every line shows its tariff item.

Short answer: Under Turkish law, in a case whose subject can be valued in money the judgment and writ fee (karar ve ilam harcı) is 68.31 per thousand of the value; one quarter of it (one twentieth in compensation claims for death or bodily injury) is paid in advance when the case is filed, together with the filing fee, and the rest within one month after service of the judgment (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a, Tariff (1) A-III-1-a). In 2026 the filing fee and the fixed judgment fee before a civil court of first instance are TRY 732 each (Fees Law General Communiqué Serial No. 98).

Information tool; not legal advice. Tariff year: 2026 (Harçlar Kanunu Genel Tebliği (Seri No: 98), Official Gazette 31 December 2025, issue 33124 (5. Mükerrer)).

How to use: choose the type of calculation (filing a case, amendment or increase of the claim, appeal, individual application to the Constitutional Court, interim injunction or precautionary attachment requested before the case), enter the date and the court; in a proportional case enter the value of the claim, then press the calculate button. The result appears on this page, just below the form. Results are preliminary estimates; the court cashier's calculation prevails.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required fields: type of calculation and date; when filing a case, the court and the type of case, and in a proportional case the value; for an amendment, the date and amount of at least one increase. Enter amounts in the Turkish number format (results are shown as 1,234.56): a dot for thousands and a comma for decimals (for example 1.234.567,89). No name, identity number or file number is requested.

1. Type of calculation and date
What should be calculated? required

The fee is charged under the tariff in force on the date of the step. Only the 2026 tariff is loaded; no amount is given for other years.

2. Case details

The tariff sets the same filing fee for all courts of first instance and the administrative court (Tariff (1) A-I-2). Tariff (3) applies in the tax court: only the filing fee is paid at filing.

Type of case

The value shown in the statement of claim (Fees Law art. 16). In actions concerning title to immovable property, the value of the property.

If chosen, the advance fee is one twentieth (Fees Law art. 28/a).

If ticked, no fee is charged (Law No. 6502 art. 73/2); the advance on costs is still calculated.

3. Amendment or increase of the claim

Enter the increased amount and the date of the increase; the fee is calculated with the tariff of the year of the increase. Up to three increases in the same case can be entered (for example an increase of a partial claim and then an amendment).

The increase, not the new total.

The bar stamp is not in the Fees Law but in art. 27 of the Attorneys' Act; enter the current amount if you know it.

The advance on costs is five times the notification fee per party plus TRY 530 (Advance on Costs Tariff art. 4). The notification fee is not in the tariff; enter the current amount.

How the calculation works

  1. Filing fee: a fixed fee charged when a case is filed, depending on the level of the court; in 2026 TRY 335.20 before a civil court of peace, TRY 732 before courts of first instance and the administrative court, TRY 1,124.50 before regional courts, the Court of Cassation and the Council of State, TRY 6,024.10 before the Constitutional Court (Tariff (1) A-I; Communiqué Serial No. 98). It is not charged again when a case is refiled with the competent court after a decision of lack of jurisdiction.
  2. Proportional judgment and writ fee: 68.31 per thousand of the value awarded in a case concerning a specific value; it cannot be lower than TRY 732 (Tariff (1) A-III-1). Where the fee depends on value, the statement of claim must show the value (Fees Law art. 16).
  3. Advance fee and balance: one quarter of the judgment and writ fee is paid in advance when the case is filed and the rest within one month after service of the judgment; in claims for pecuniary and non-pecuniary damages for death or bodily injury the advance share is one twentieth (Fees Law art. 28/a). Non-payment of the balance does not prevent service of the judgment, enforcement or an appeal.
  4. Fixed judgment fee: TRY 732 in 2026 in cases not subject to the proportional fee and on dismissal; fixed fees are paid in advance (Tariff (1) A-III-2-a; Fees Law art. 27). TRY 1,206 on interim injunction and precautionary attachment decisions (A-III-2-d), TRY 5,188 for an on-site inspection (A-V).
  5. Amendment and increase of the claim: the advance fee is paid on the increased value; until it is paid the next step is not taken (Fees Law art. 30, 32). An amendment (ıslah) can be made once in the same case until the end of the evidentiary stage (Code of Civil Procedure art. 176, 177); in a partial claim the amount can be increased once without the bar on expanding the claim (art. 109/4). Art. 107 on claims of indefinite amount was repealed by Law No. 7589. An amendment whose fee is unpaid cannot be relied on; if the fee is not paid within the time limit granted by the court, the judgment is given on the amount in the statement of claim (Court of Cassation 20th Civil Chamber, 01.12.2016, 2015/6619 E., 2016/11583 K.).
  6. Appeals: TRY 2,002 for an appeal (istinaf) to a regional court of appeal or regional administrative court and TRY 3,608.50 for an appeal on points of law (temyiz) to the Court of Cassation or the Council of State (Tariff (1) A-IV); the filing fee is in A-I-3.
  7. Power of attorney fee and advance on costs: TRY 104 for a copy of the power of attorney certified by the lawyer (Tariff (1) D-I-c). The advance on costs is five times the notification fee per party plus TRY 530 (Code of Civil Procedure art. 120; Advance on Costs Tariff art. 4, Official Gazette 28.10.2025, issue 33061). The bar stamp is based on art. 27 of the Attorneys' Act; its amount is entered manually.
  8. Rounding: no rule on rounding the proportional fee to the kuruş was found in the Fees Law or the communiqué; each item is rounded to the kuruş (half a kuruş rounded up). The court cashier's calculation prevails.

What is not included

  • Notification fees, fees of court-appointed assessors (bilirkişi) and witness fees; for the advance on costs only the tariff formula is used, and the notification fee is the amount you enter.
  • The proportional judgment fee in appeals, hearing fees, criminal court fees and the security required for a precautionary attachment.
  • Applying exemptions (Fees Law art. 13; special laws) and legal aid (Code of Civil Procedure art. 334–336): the calculator only shows them as notes.
  • Years other than 2026: no amount is given for a year without a loaded tariff.
  • No personal data is requested; the calculation is made in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advance fee and how is it calculated?

In a case whose subject can be valued in money, the court charges a judgment and writ fee of 68.31 per thousand of the value awarded (Tariff (1) A-III-1-a of Fees Law No. 492). One quarter of this fee is paid in advance when the case is filed (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a). For a claim of TRY 100,000 the proportional fee is TRY 6,831 and the advance fee TRY 1,707.75. The proportional fee cannot be lower than TRY 732.

What is the difference between a proportional and a fixed fee?

A proportional (nispi) fee is charged in proportion to the value of the matter; a fixed (maktu) fee is a set amount depending on the type of the matter (Fees Law No. 492 art. 15). Cases concerning a specific value pay the proportional judgment fee; other cases (for example divorce, declaratory actions, dismissals) pay the fixed judgment fee, TRY 732 in 2026 (Tariff (1) A-III-2-a). Where the fee depends on value, the statement of claim must show the value (art. 16).

Why is the advance fee one twentieth in bodily injury cases?

Under Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a, in claims for pecuniary and non-pecuniary damages for death or bodily injury the advance fee is one twentieth instead of one quarter. For a TRY 10,000,000 work accident claim the proportional fee is TRY 683,100 and the advance fee TRY 34,155; the remaining TRY 648,945 is paid after the judgment according to the amount awarded.

When is the balance of the judgment fee paid?

The judgment and writ fee beyond the advance part is paid within one month after service of the judgment (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a). Non-payment of the balance does not prevent service of the judgment, enforcement or an appeal (same article). If the advance fee exceeds the fee finally due, the excess is refunded on request (art. 31).

Which fee is paid when the claim is amended or increased?

When the value of the case is increased, the advance judgment fee (one quarter of 68.31 per thousand; one twentieth in bodily injury cases) is paid on the increased amount; until the fee is paid, the next procedural step is not taken (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a, 30, 32). According to the 20th Civil Chamber of the Court of Cassation (01.12.2016, 2015/6619 E., 2016/11583 K.), an amendment whose fee is unpaid cannot be relied on; the court grants a time limit and, if the fee is not paid, rules on the amount in the statement of claim. An amendment (ıslah) can be made once in the same case until the end of the evidentiary stage (Code of Civil Procedure art. 176, 177); in a partial claim the amount can be increased once (art. 109/4).

Who is exempt from court fees, and what does legal aid cover?

Cases and enforcement proceedings with a value of up to TRY 50 (except those based on commercial papers), the appointment and removal of guardians and cases brought by public prosecutors on behalf of the public are exempt from fees, as are the transactions of general budget administrations under Tariffs (1) and (3) (Fees Law No. 492 art. 13). A case brought by a consumer in the consumer court is also exempt from fees (Law No. 6502 art. 73/2). A legal aid decision provides temporary exemption from litigation and enforcement costs until the judgment becomes final (Code of Civil Procedure art. 335).
