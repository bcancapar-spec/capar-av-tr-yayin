Bodily Injury (Physical Harm) Compensation Calculator under Turkish Law: Work Accident, Traffic Accident

Go to the calculator

Estimates, under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye), the pecuniary compensation of a person who suffers permanent incapacity for work (incapacity, maluliyet) in a work accident, a traffic accident or another tort, using the actuarial method of Court of Cassation practice: remaining life expectancy under the TRH 2010 life table, known and unknown periods, active and passive periods, the recovery period, reduction for fault, set-off of the recoverable part of the payments of the Social Security Institution (SGK) and, in a traffic accident, the insurer's per-person limit. It shows each step with its statutory provision and Court of Cassation decision; the PMF 1931 table appears for comparison only.

Short answer: In the practice of the Court of Cassation under Turkish law, pecuniary compensation for bodily injury (cismani zarar) is the share, equal to the incapacity (maluliyet) rate, of the net earnings lost over the remaining life expectancy found in the TRH 2010 table for the age at the accident date; during the recovery (rest) period the loss is taken as 100%. In a work accident the active period runs to age 60 for men and age 55 for women, and in traffic accidents and other bodily injury to age 60 for both, and is calculated on the wage; the passive period that follows is calculated on the net minimum wage without the minimum living allowance (AGİ, asgari geçim indirimi). A reduction is made for the injured person's own fault, the part of the payments of the Social Security Institution (SGK) that can be recovered by recourse is deducted, and in a traffic accident the insurer's liability is limited to the per-person limit on the accident date (Turkish Code of Obligations (TBK) art. 52, 54, 55; Highway Traffic Law (KTK) art. 85, 91; Law No. 5510 art. 21).

How the calculation is made

  1. Remaining life expectancy: the age on the accident date is rounded to the nearest whole age, and the remaining life expectancy in the TRH 2010 life table (separate for women and men) is added to find the probable end of life. The age on the report date is not used (Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2018/1027, K. 2021/1708; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/7201, K. 2024/10400; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/12220, K. 2026/1712). The PMF 1931 table appears in the result for comparison only.
  2. Active and passive period: in a work accident the wage multiplier is applied during the active period, up to age 60 for men and age 55 for women, and the net minimum wage during the passive period that follows, to the end of life; for a woman worker the amount found with age 60 is also shown. If an old-age or disability pension was granted earlier, the active period ends on that date (10th Civil Chamber, E. 2022/9984, K. 2024/2203; E. 2024/9776, K. 2025/5232; E. 2025/13923, K. 2026/1377). In traffic accidents and other bodily injury the active period ends at age 60 for both women and men; in the passive period the minimum wage without AGİ is used (4th Civil Chamber, E. 2025/9068, K. 2025/15079; E. 2026/4122, K. 2026/6056).
  3. Wage multiplier and minimum living allowance: the net wage on the accident date is divided by the net minimum wage on the same date; the multiplier is applied to every minimum wage period (10th Civil Chamber, E. 2026/6276, K. 2026/5229). In traffic accidents and other bodily injury, for a person actually working at the accident, the minimum living allowance (AGİ) is added to the base of the active period for periods up to and including 2021; for a person not working it is never added; for a child under 18 who is not working, the minimum wage without AGİ is used up to age 18 and the minimum wage including AGİ afterwards (4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/9762, K. 2025/5480; E. 2022/16716, K. 2024/1897).
  4. Known period: for each minimum wage period from the accident date to the calculation date, days × daily wage (one thirtieth of the monthly wage); no increase or discount is applied.
  5. Unknown period: from the calculation date to the end of life; because the annual wage increase and the discount are taken at the same rate (mostly 10% in practice; 5% in some decisions of the 10th Civil Chamber), annual earnings stay constant; in a traffic accident the 1.8% technical interest rate is not applied (Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2018/1027, K. 2021/1708; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/1913, K. 2026/1414; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/2690, K. 2025/3146; 17th Civil Chamber, E. 2019/6425, K. 2021/2044).
  6. Incapacity and recovery period: earnings are multiplied by the incapacity rate; on the days of treatment, rest or recovery the loss is taken as 100% (TBK art. 54; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/2873, K. 2024/7513; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/5671, K. 2025/2602). For a person not working in an income-generating job at the accident, no loss arises for this period (4th Civil Chamber, E. 2026/4122, K. 2026/6056).
  7. Reduction for fault: the total loss is reduced in proportion to the injured person's fault (TBK art. 52/1; KTK art. 86/2). In a traffic accident, if the gratuitous carriage option is ticked for a passenger, a 20% reduction is made separately from the temporary and the permanent incapacity loss after the fault reduction; the SGK set-off and the comparison with the limit use the reduced amount (TBK art. 51; KTK art. 87/1; Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/377, K. 2025/783; E. 2023/5936, K. 2025/3688).
  8. Set-off: in a work accident, the initial capitalised value of the income granted by SGK and the other recoverable payments, multiplied by the other party's share of fault, are deducted from the permanent incapacity loss, and the temporary incapacity allowance, multiplied by the same rate, from the loss of the rest period; a part exceeding one head is not deducted from the other. The employer's payment on account of the compensation is deducted as it is (TBK art. 55/1; Law No. 5510 art. 21/1; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2020/6776, K. 2020/5620; E. 2023/2873, K. 2024/7513; E. 2025/13923, K. 2026/1377). In traffic accidents and other bodily injury, the amounts SGK treats as recoverable are deducted from their own heads (Law No. 5510 art. 21/4; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/12943, K. 2025/5819).
  9. Traffic insurance: the insurer is liable up to the per-person bodily injury limit on the accident date; compensation for temporary and permanent incapacity is assessed together within this limit; for the part exceeding it, other liable persons such as the vehicle operator and the driver at fault are jointly and severally liable (KTK art. 85/1, 88/1, 91/1; Regulation on Tariff Implementation Principles art. 24/1, 28/A; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2022/14422, K. 2025/314).

Not included in the calculation

  • Loss-of-support compensation in fatal accidents (TBK art. 53): it requires further assumptions on support shares and the beneficiaries.
  • Carer costs, treatment costs and non-pecuniary damages. Non-pecuniary damages are set by the judge at discretion (TBK art. 56).
  • The reduction for conduct that increases the loss, such as not wearing a seat belt or helmet or riding with a drunk or unlicensed driver (contributory fault, TBK art. 52/1; taken into account by the court of its own motion; 20% in the practice of the Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber): the tool does not take it into account. The gratuitous carriage (hatır taşıması) reduction (TBK art. 51) is applied only if selected.
  • Updating pre-action payments before deducting them; the narrowing of the limit by payments the insurer makes to SGK or to other injured persons, and the per-accident limit.
  • Interest, equitable reduction (TBK art. 52/2), unavoidability in a work accident and the fault of third persons other than the employer. For work accidents on or before 31.07.2026, in the practice of the Court of Cassation interest runs on the whole compensation from the accident date; for later events TBK art. 55/3 applies.
  • For events after 31.07.2026, the proportional set-off of payments made before the investigation phase begins (TBK art. 55/4; Law No. 7589 provisional art. 1/9).
  • Limitation: a compensation claim arising from a tort or a traffic accident becomes time-barred two years after the injured person learns of the loss and of the person liable, and in any case ten years after the event; if the act is a crime subject to a longer criminal limitation period, that period applies (TBK art. 72; KTK art. 109). For claims based on an employment contract the general ten-year period (TBK art. 146) comes into play. The tool does not check limitation.
  • In a traffic accident a written application to the insurer is required before filing a lawsuit; if the insurer does not reply in writing within 15 days or the reply does not meet the claim, a lawsuit may be filed or arbitration may be sought (KTK art. 97; Constitutional Court E.2019/40 K.2020/40). If the vehicle was uninsured or the insured cannot be identified, bodily injury losses may be claimed from the Güvence Hesabı (the compensation fund for uninsured vehicles) up to the applicable limits (Insurance Law No. 5684 art. 14).
  • No personal data is requested; the calculation is made in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the bodily injury compensation calculator used?

It works in three modes: work accident (the method of the Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber), and traffic accident and other bodily injury (the method of the 4th Civil Chamber; for example injury caused by a tort). It estimates pecuniary compensation for permanent and temporary incapacity for work and, in the traffic mode, also shows the per-person limit of compulsory traffic insurance. Non-pecuniary damages, carer and treatment costs, interest and loss-of-support compensation in fatal accidents are outside its scope. Basis: TBK art. 54, 55; KTK art. 85, 91.

How is compensation for permanent incapacity for work calculated in a work accident?

The net earnings the worker would have obtained from the accident date to the probable end of life are found period by period and multiplied by the incapacity rate (loss of earning capacity in the occupation); during the days of treatment and rest the loss is taken as 100%. The worker's fault share is deducted from the total, and then the part of the SGK payments that can be recovered by recourse and the payments the employer made on account of the compensation are deducted. Basis: TBK art. 52, 54, 55; Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2021/5965, K. 2022/10869.

Which table and which date determine the remaining life expectancy?

In place of the old PMF 1931 table, the Court of Cassation applies the TRH 2010 life table, which is specific to Türkiye and separate for women and men; the remaining life expectancy is found from the age at the accident date, not at the report date. In work accidents no distinction is made by the date of the event (Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2017/1179, K. 2021/1563: accident of 2009); in traffic accidents the Assembly of Civil Chambers applied PMF 1931 to an event of 2010 (E. 2024/643, K. 2025/264, by majority) and TRH 2010 to an event of 2017 (E. 2025/488, K. 2026/223). The calculator uses TRH 2010; PMF 1931 is shown for comparison only. Basis: Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2018/1027, K. 2021/1708; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/12220, K. 2026/1712.

How does the difference between PMF 1931 and TRH 2010 affect the result?

PMF 1931 is an old table that makes no distinction by gender and shows a shorter remaining life expectancy than TRH 2010. For example, for a person exactly 30 years old at the accident date, the remaining life expectancy is 37.50 years under PMF 1931, and 44.45 years for men and 49.00 years for women under TRH 2010. The longer the remaining life expectancy, the longer the passive period and therefore the higher the compensation. The result screen shows the values of the two tables side by side; the calculation is made with TRH 2010. Basis: Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2025/488, K. 2026/223; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/12309, K. 2024/13137.

What do the active period and the passive period mean?

The active period is the period in which the injured person is assumed to earn income by working, and it is calculated on the wage; in the passive period that follows, the loss is taken at the level of the net minimum wage without the minimum living allowance (AGİ). In work accidents the Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber takes age 60 for men and age 55 for women in the worker's own action; the calculator also shows the amount found with age 60 for a woman worker, and ends the active period on the start date of an old-age or disability pension granted before age 60. In traffic accidents and other bodily injury the 4th Civil Chamber takes age 60 for both women and men. Basis: Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/9776, K. 2025/5232; E. 2025/13923, K. 2026/1377; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2025/9068, K. 2025/15079; E. 2026/4122, K. 2026/6056.

What are the known and unknown periods, and how are increase and discount applied?

In the known period, from the accident date to the calculation (report) date, the actual amounts of each minimum wage period are used without increase or discount. What follows is the unknown period; here the annual wage increase and the discount for advance payment are taken at the same rate (mostly 10% in practice; 5% in some decisions of the 10th Civil Chamber), so annual earnings stay constant. Basis: Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2018/1027, K. 2021/1708; 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2024/2690, K. 2025/3146.

How does a wage above the minimum wage affect the calculation?

The net wage on the accident date is divided by the net minimum wage without the minimum living allowance on that date, and the resulting multiplier (for example 1.45) is applied to every minimum wage period of the active period. The calculator treats a wage below the minimum wage as the minimum wage; no multiplier is applied in the passive period. Basis: Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2021/5965, K. 2022/10869; E. 2026/6276, K. 2026/5229.

How is bodily injury compensation calculated in a traffic accident?

The Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber applies the TRH 2010 table and the progressive annuity method (10% annual increase and 10% discount); the 1.8% technical interest rate is not used. For a person who was working in an income-generating job at the accident, the minimum living allowance is added to the base of the active period for periods up to and including 2021; no AGİ is added in the passive period. The loss for the recovery period and the permanent incapacity loss are reduced by the injured person's own share of fault, and the insurer is liable only up to the per-person limit on the accident date. Basis: 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/1913, K. 2026/1414; E. 2023/9762, K. 2025/5480; E. 2023/2514, K. 2025/1018; 17th Civil Chamber, E. 2019/6425, K. 2021/2044.

Does traffic insurance (ZMSS) pay the whole compensation?

The insurer is liable up to the per-person bodily injury limit in force on the accident date; for example TRY 330,000 for an accident in 2017 and TRY 3,600,000 for an accident in 2026. Compensation for temporary and permanent incapacity for work is assessed together within this limit. For the part exceeding the limit, other liable persons such as the vehicle operator and the driver at fault are jointly and severally liable. For vehicles engaged in intercity or international carriage the limit has been doubled since 26.04.2016. Basis: KTK art. 85/1, 88/1, 91/1; Regulation on Tariff Implementation Principles (Tarife Uygulama Esasları Hakkında Yönetmelik) art. 24/1, 28/A; Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2022/14422, K. 2025/314.

Is the compensation reduced for gratuitous carriage (hatır taşıması)?

If a passenger is carried free of charge (as a favour), the judge may reduce the compensation under TBK art. 51. The reduction is made if the other party raised the gratuitous carriage defence in time; in its settled practice the Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber applies a rate of 20% and compares the reduced amount with the insurance limit. Gratuitous carriage is not accepted in the carriage of close relatives and spouses. The reduction is made after the fault reduction and before pre-action payments are deducted. The calculator applies this reduction only if you select it. Basis: Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/377, K. 2025/783; E. 2023/5936, K. 2025/3688; E. 2023/5826, K. 2025/3662; E. 2024/4097, K. 2024/5762; E. 2025/4638, K. 2025/9805.

How is compensation calculated for a person who is not working or for a child?

For a person who is not actually working in an income-generating job, compensation for loss of working capacity is calculated on the minimum wage without AGİ, and no temporary incapacity loss arises because no income is lost during the recovery period. For a child under 18 at the accident date, the minimum wage without AGİ is used up to age 18 and the minimum wage including AGİ in the active period after 18. Basis: Court of Cassation 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/9762, K. 2025/5480; E. 2022/16716, K. 2024/1897; E. 2023/11711, K. 2023/13013; E. 2026/4122, K. 2026/6056.

Is the income granted by SGK deducted from the compensation?

Yes, but only the part that can be recovered by recourse and only from the relevant head of loss. In a work accident, the initial capitalised value of the income granted by SGK, multiplied by the other party's share of fault, is deducted from the permanent incapacity loss, and the temporary incapacity allowance, multiplied by the same rate, from the loss of the rest period. In a work accident caused by the fault of a third party (for example a traffic accident that is also a work accident), SGK may recover the payments and half of the initial capitalised value of the income; in these modes the calculator directly deducts the amount SGK treats as recoverable. Payments that cannot be recovered cannot be deducted from the compensation. Basis: TBK art. 55/1; Law No. 5510 art. 21/1, 21/4; Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2020/6776, K. 2020/5620; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/12943, K. 2025/5819.

How does the injured person's own fault affect the compensation?

If the injured person is at fault in causing the accident, the calculated loss is reduced by that share of fault. Separately from fault in causing the accident, for conduct that increases the loss, such as not wearing a seat belt or helmet or riding with a drunk or unlicensed driver (contributory fault), the court makes a further reduction; in the practice of the 4th Civil Chamber the rate is 20%, and the calculator does not take it into account (4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/377, K. 2025/783). In a traffic accident, compensation claims corresponding to the injured person's own fault fall outside compulsory traffic insurance. The set-off of SGK payments is made after this reduction. Basis: TBK art. 52/1; KTK art. 86/2, 92/g; Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2021/5965, K. 2022/10869.

How is the treatment and rest (temporary incapacity for work) period calculated?

On the days when the injured person is under treatment and unable to work, the loss is calculated on the wage with the loss of working capacity taken as 100%, not at the incapacity rate; the permanent incapacity loss starts from the end of this period. The recoverable part of the temporary incapacity allowance that SGK paid for this period is deducted from the loss of this period. In work accidents, rest days falling after the end of the active period (after age 55 for a woman worker) are also calculated on the wage. Even if the incapacity rate is 0%, the loss for this period arises; in that case the calculator calculates only this loss. No such loss arises for a person who was not working at the accident. Basis: Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2025/8113, K. 2025/15059; E. 2023/2873, K. 2024/7513; 4th Civil Chamber, E. 2023/5671, K. 2025/2602; E. 2026/4122, K. 2026/6056; Law No. 5510 art. 18.

If the incapacity rate is below 60%, is a passive period calculated after age 60?

In the recourse actions that SGK brings against the employer, the Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber does not take the passive period loss into account where incapacity is below 60%. In the worker's own compensation action the passive period is calculated; as different assessments exist on this point, however, the calculator also shows a result excluding the passive period for rates below 60%. Basis: Court of Cassation 10th Civil Chamber, E. 2025/11479, K. 2025/16857; E. 2025/8954, K. 2026/1731; E. 2025/15139, K. 2026/6369.

How is the calculation made for other bodily injury (for example injury caused by a tort)?

In torts other than traffic accidents too, bodily injury is calculated under TBK art. 54 and 55 on the same actuarial principles: TRH 2010, active period to age 60, passive period at the minimum wage without AGİ, a reduction for the injured person's share of fault and deduction of the recoverable SGK payments. There is no insurance limit or gratuitous carriage in this mode. Depending on the type of dispute, the competent court, chamber or avenue of appeal may differ. Basis: TBK art. 49, 54, 55; Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2023/1155, K. 2025/263.

Can non-pecuniary damages be calculated as well?

No. Non-pecuniary damages are not the result of a calculation; the judge sets an appropriate amount taking into account the circumstances of the event, the shares of fault, the degree of incapacity and the situation of the parties. This tool estimates only pecuniary compensation. Basis: TBK art. 56.

What changed in bodily injury compensation with Law No. 7589?

For events occurring after 31.07.2026, statutory interest runs on the compensation for the known period from the date of the event and on the compensation for the unknown period from the date of the decision; a payment made for the purpose of performance until the investigation phase (tahkikat) begins is set off proportionally against the compensation determined according to the date of payment. The calculator deducts payments made after the investigation phase begins as they are. For earlier work accidents, in the practice of the Court of Cassation interest runs on the whole compensation from the accident date. HMK art. 107, which regulated the action for an undetermined claim, has been repealed; it continues to apply to actions filed before the repeal. Basis: TBK art. 55/3-4; Law No. 7589 provisional art. 1/9, 1/10.

Can loss-of-support compensation in a fatal accident be calculated with this tool?

No. A loss-of-support calculation requires further assumptions such as support shares, the ages of the beneficiaries, the probability of the spouse's remarriage and the ages at which children cease to be dependent, so it is outside the scope of this tool. The tool estimates only permanent and temporary incapacity compensation. Basis: TBK art. 53; Court of Cassation Assembly of Civil Chambers, E. 2018/1027, K. 2021/1708.

To calculate the court fee for this amount, use the Court Fee Calculator: in compensation claims for death or bodily injury the advance fee is one twentieth (Fees Law No. 492 art. 28/a). The amount is not carried in the address bar; you enter the value in the fee calculator yourself.
