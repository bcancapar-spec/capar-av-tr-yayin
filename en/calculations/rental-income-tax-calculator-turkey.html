Rental Income Tax Calculator for Türkiye: Residential Exemption, Expenses, Withholding, Filing Limits and Instalments

Go to the calculator

Enter the income year, the residential and commercial rent collected and the expense method: under Turkish law (the law of the Republic of Türkiye) the calculator applies the residential exemption and filing limits, deducts expenses, calculates the income tax by bracket, sets off the tax withheld by the tenant and shows the March and July instalments (Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 21, 74, 86, 103, 117).

Short answer: How it works under Turkish law: the exemption amount of yearly residential rent (47,000 TL for 2025 income, 58,000 TL for 2026) is not taxed and the excess is declared. Expenses are deducted as a lump sum (15%) or as documented actual expenses; for 2025 onwards, mortgage interest on a home let as a residence is not deductible. Commercial rent from which the tenant withheld tax is not declared if the gross amount does not exceed the second bracket. The tax is paid in March and July of the following year (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 21, 74, 86, 103, 117).

How the calculation works

  1. Rent: enter the rent collected in the year; rent for future years collected in advance belongs to the year it relates to (art. 72).
  2. Residential exemption: the yearly exemption is deducted; those with a commercial income return and those whose income exceeds the third bracket amount for wages cannot use it (art. 21).
  3. Filing limits: commercial rent with tax withheld is not declared if it does not exceed the second bracket amount, rent without withholding if it does not exceed the yearly limit (art. 86).
  4. Expenses: 15% lump sum or documented actual expenses; expenses attributable to the exemption are not deducted; for 2025 and later income mortgage interest on a residence is not deducted (art. 74; Law No. 7566 art. 1).
  5. Tax and payment: the net income is taxed at the rates, the withheld tax is set off; return in March of the following year, two instalments in March and July (art. 92, art. 103, art. 117).

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to file a return for rental income in Türkiye?

If residential rent exceeds the yearly exemption, the excess is declared; otherwise no return is needed. Commercial rent with tax withheld by the tenant is not declared if the gross amount plus other income subject to withholding does not exceed the second bracket (400,000 TL in 2026); commercial rent without withholding is declared if it exceeds the yearly limit (22,000 TL in 2026) (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 21, art. 86).

Who cannot use the residential rent exemption?

Those obliged to file an annual return for commercial, agricultural or professional income, and those who earn residential rent above the exemption and whose gross salary, interest, dividends, rent and other income exceed the amount in the third bracket for wages (1,500,000 TL in 2026). A person who does not declare, or under-declares, rent above the exemption also loses it (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 21).

Lump-sum or actual expenses: which is better?

With the lump sum, 15% of the declared rent is deducted without documents; with actual expenses, documented costs such as service charges, insurance, repairs and depreciation are deducted and the part attributable to the exemption cannot be deducted. Whoever chooses the lump sum cannot return to actual expenses for two years; those letting rights cannot choose the lump sum (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 74).

Can mortgage interest be deducted from rental income?

Law No. 7566 added the words 'except residences' to GVK art. 74/1-4: for 2025 and later income, interest on debt for property let as a residence is not deductible; for commercial property the deduction continues. The deduction of 5% of the acquisition cost of a residence remains in the text (Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 74; Law No. 7566 art. 1, art. 38).

Is short-term letting through platforms such as Airbnb rental income?

The Council of State (3rd Chamber) held that letting property daily or weekly through a platform, without a commercial organisation offering services such as breakfast or cleaning like a hotel or guesthouse, does not change its character as rental income (E.2024/6160, K.2025/1856; Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 70, art. 72).

In which year is rent collected in advance for a later year taxed?

Rental income is taxed on a cash basis, but rent for future years collected in advance counts as income of the years it relates to. The Council of State (3rd Chamber) upheld moving rent collected in advance for the next year to that year (E.2020/134, K.2022/3462; Income Tax Law No. 193 (GVK) art. 72).
