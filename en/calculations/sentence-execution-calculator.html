Sentence Execution Calculator

Go to the calculator

Calculates, under Turkish law, the dates of conditional release (koşullu salıverilme), probation (denetimli serbestlik), transfer to an open penal institution (açık ceza infaz kurumu), release on completion of the sentence (hak ederek tahliye) and the end of the supervision period (denetim süresi) for final prison sentences, according to Law No. 5275 on the Execution of Penalties and Security Measures and the related regulations; it shows each date with its legal basis and a step-by-step calculation. Where the text does not regulate a matter, it gives no date and says so.

Short answer: The execution of prison sentences under Turkish law is calculated according to Law No. 5275 on the Execution of Penalties and Security Measures (Ceza ve Güvenlik Tedbirlerinin İnfazı Hakkında Kanun). For fixed-term imprisonment, conditional release (koşullu salıverilme) as a rule requires that half of the sentence has been served in a penal institution with good conduct; for listed offences such as intentional killing the ratio is two thirds, and for drug trafficking and certain sexual offences, terror offences and second-time recidivism it is three quarters. A convict of good conduct in an open penal institution may benefit from probation (denetimli serbestlik) when one year remains until conditional release; for offences committed before 30 March 2020 and 31 July 2023, the temporary articles of the Law provide longer periods. Time spent in police custody and pre-trial detention is deducted from the sentence. The calculator's result is an estimate; the official dates are set out in the time-calculation document (süre belgesi).

Information tool; not legal advice.

This is the English version of the Turkish calculator İnfaz Hesaplama; the Turkish text is the original. Both pages use the same calculation engine and the same rules under Turkish law. Article references use the Turkish abbreviations, for example TCK (Turkish Criminal Code), CMK (Code of Criminal Procedure) and CBY (Regulation on the Management of Penal Institutions); "art. 107/2" means Article 107, paragraph 2.

How to use: fill in the required fields, open the optional sections that fit your situation and press the calculate button; the result appears on this page, just below the form. Results are preliminary estimates.

The information you enter is calculated in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Required fields: at least one judgment, the convict's date of birth and the start of execution. The other sections are optional; a field left empty is not taken into account. No personal data, case number or court name is requested.

Calculated dates

  • Conditional release (koşullu salıverilme): the day on which the part of the sentence set by law has been served in the penal institution with good conduct (5275 art. 107, art. 108; 3713 art. 17).
  • Start of probation (denetimli serbestlik): the earliest day on which a convict of good conduct in an open institution may begin serving the time remaining until conditional release under the probation measure (5275 art. 105/A; temporary art. 6, art. 10/6, art. 11).
  • Transfer to an open penal institution: the earliest day of direct admission to an open institution or of transfer from a closed to an open institution (5275 art. 14; Open Penal Institutions Regulation art. 6–8; temporary art. 10/6).
  • Release on completion of the sentence (hak ederek tahliye): the day on which the whole sentence has been executed (TCK art. 61/6, art. 63; CBY art. 54/3-d).
  • End of the supervision period and of the deprivation of rights: the supervision period equals the period to be served in the institution for conditional release and, for fixed-term imprisonment, may not go beyond the date of release on completion of the sentence; if it is spent with good conduct, the sentence is deemed executed (5275 art. 107/6, art. 107/14). Deprivations of rights arise only on a prison sentence for an intentional offence and for a person who had reached the age of eighteen when committing the act (TCK art. 53/1, art. 53/2, art. 53/4).
  • Revocation of conditional release: if an intentional offence is committed during the supervision period, conditional release is revoked; twice the prison sentence imposed for that offence is served in the institution, starting from the date of the offence and not going beyond the date of release on completion of the sentence. As the text does not make clear whether this period is a date range or a period to be spent in the institution, no end date is given; only the legal basis is shown (5275 art. 107/12–15).
  • Later judgment: a new judgment that becomes final while the person is in prison or on probation is aggregated with the earlier sentence; days on probation count as execution, and the open institution and probation cannot be earlier than the date of readmission to prison. For a new judgment received while on conditional release (one that does not lead to revocation), no date is given, because the method of aggregation and the order of revocation and the new sentence are not regulated in the text; if execution of the earlier sentence is complete, the new judgment is calculated on its own (5275 art. 99/1, art. 105/A, art. 107/12–14).

Order of calculation

  1. Units of time: a month is thirty days and a year is a calendar year (TCK art. 61/6); a year added to 29 February ends on 28 February. The end of a period is found by adding the period to the start day. When periods are added up or ratios applied, twelve months count as one calendar year; «up to 30/3/2020» in temporary art. 6 includes 30.03.2020 (the text is not explicit on these two points; the rule applied is stated in the result).
  2. Deduction (mahsup): days in police custody and pre-trial detention (TCK art. 63), days spent abroad for the same offence (TCK art. 16) and, from 14.07.2021, one day for every two days under the obligation not to leave the residence (CMK art. 109/6) are counted back from the start of execution; the calculation starts from that day.
  3. Ratio: each judgment gets its own ratio; a fractional day resulting from the ratio is rounded up. With more than one judgment the periods are added together and the upper limit is applied (5275 art. 99/1, art. 107/3).
  4. Age: each day spent in the institution until the age of 15 counts as two days; for offences committed up to 30.03.2020, three days until 15 and two days until 18. This rule does not apply to intentional killing, art. 102, art. 103, art. 188 and forming an organisation to commit crimes (art. 220) committed on or after 18.08.2026 (5275 art. 107/5; temporary art. 6/4).
  5. Open institution and probation: time in the institution starts with pre-trial detention (and custody together with detention); with more than one judgment, probation is found separately for each judgment and the earliest eligible date is taken.
  6. Discipline: a disciplinary penalty that has not been lifted postpones the open institution, probation and the conditional release procedure until the date on which it is lifted; the conditional release procedure may not go beyond the date of release on completion of the sentence (5275 art. 48/3-b).
  7. More favourable law: amendments to the conditional release ratio in favour of the convict also apply to earlier offences (TCK art. 7/2); if the date found with the new ratio falls before the amendment's entry into force, the date found with the old text in force on the date of the offence is used if it is earlier, otherwise the entry-into-force date. An amendment extending the supervision period applies only to offences committed on or after 15.04.2020.

Points the text does not regulate: no date given

  • How many days a weekend or a night counts for in weekend and night execution (5275 art. 110/1): only eligibility is shown.
  • Periods for transferring child convicts to a children's education centre: the regulation required by 5275 art. 15/6 could not be found.
  • Days spent under the obligation not to leave the residence before 14.07.2021: Law No. 7331 contains no transitional provision.
  • Police custody and pre-trial detention in another case: not counted unless its deduction has been accepted in the execution file.
  • The temporary-article date where only some of several judgments fall within temporary art. 10/6; the end of the supervision period for judgments with offence dates on both sides of 15.04.2020.
  • Discretionary periods: breach of obligations on revocation (5275 art. 107/13-b), extension of a recidivist's supervision (art. 108/6), leave and the decision on good conduct.
  • The end of the period to be served on revocation of conditional release and its order with the new sentence (5275 art. 107/13-a); the aggregation of sentences for a new judgment received while on conditional release that does not lead to revocation.

Not calculated

  • Suspended prison sentences or sentences converted into alternative sanctions (TCK art. 50, art. 51) and cases of deferral of the announcement of the judgment (hükmün açıklanmasının geri bırakılması) are not the subject of an execution calculation.
  • Deferral of execution because of military service (5275 art. 117; Law No. 1111 art. 39), and decisions to postpone or interrupt execution; for these, the days spent outside execution can be entered in the «Days spent outside execution» field.
  • Offences committed before 1 June 2005, dates of excuse leave and special leave, coercive imprisonment and disciplinary imprisonment.
  • Deferral of execution under art. 6 of Law No. 7595 for offences related to the PKK/KCK terrorist organisation: it depends on the publication of the National Security Council decision in the Official Gazette and on the convict's written notification (Law No. 7595 art. 1, 9); when a terrorism or organisation offence is selected, a note is shown in the result.
  • The official dates are set out in the time-calculation document (süre belgesi) issued by the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office (5275 art. 20/4; CBY art. 54/3); if there is doubt in the calculation, the deciding authority is the enforcement judge (infaz hâkimliği; 5275 art. 98/1-b).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much of the sentence must be served in a penal institution for conditional release?

For fixed-term imprisonment the rule is one half; for offences such as intentional killing, torture, certain sexual offences, offences against private life and espionage it is two thirds; for the offences in articles 102/2, 103, 104/2-3 and 188, terror offences and second-time recidivism it is three quarters. For life imprisonment it is 24 years and for aggravated life imprisonment 30 years. Legal basis: 5275 art. 107/2, 108/3, 108/9; 3713 art. 17.

When does probation (denetimli serbestlik) start?

A convict of good conduct in an open penal institution or a children's education centre may apply when one year or less remains until conditional release; the decision rests with the enforcement judge (infaz hâkimi). Legal basis: 5275 art. 105/A/1.

Is the probation period different for offences committed before 30 March 2020?

Except for the offences listed in the Law, the one-year period is applied as three years; for a woman with a child aged zero to six and for convicts who have completed the age of 70 it is four years. Legal basis: 5275 temporary art. 6.

What changes for offences committed on or before 31 July 2023?

Except for the listed exceptions, transfer to an open institution and probation may be obtained three years earlier; for probation, at least three months must be spent in an open institution. Legal basis: 5275 temporary art. 10/6.

Is time spent in police custody and pre-trial detention deducted from the sentence?

Periods spent in situations restricting personal liberty before the judgment became final are deducted from the prison sentence; under the obligation not to leave the residence (house arrest), every two days from 14.07.2021 count as one day. Legal basis: TCK art. 63; CMK art. 109/6.

Is the calculator's result official?

No; the official dates are set out in the time-calculation document (süre belgesi) that the Chief Public Prosecutor's Office issues to the convict. Legal basis: 5275 art. 20/4; CBY art. 54/3.
