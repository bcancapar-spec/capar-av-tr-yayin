Abatement action Tenkis

A lawsuit brought by heirs who have not received the value of their reserved portions, to reduce the deceased's dispositions that exceed the disposable part to the statutory limit.

Action for annulment (administrative courts) İptal davası (idari yargı)

An administrative lawsuit brought by a person whose interests are affected, seeking to set aside an administrative act as unlawful in terms of competence, form, grounds, subject or purpose. Action for cancellation and re-registration of title Tapu iptali ve tescil davası

A lawsuit claiming that a land registry entry is unlawful and seeking its cancellation and registration of the property in the name of the true owner; the person whose right in rem is impaired may request correction of the register, while rights acquired by third parties in good faith are protected.
