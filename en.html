About Us

Çapar Law Office was founded in 2001 by Att. Bekir Çapar in Denizli; its founding attorney Bekir Çapar now serves as the Notary of Aliağa (first-class notary), Türkiye. Since its establishment, the office has served its individual and corporate clients with an approach grounded in trust, diligence, experience, knowledge and risk management.

Av. Bayram Can ÇAPAR is a graduate of the Yaşar University Faculty of Law. Registered with the Denizli Bar, he has practised since 2017 as the founding attorney of ÇAPAR Hukuk and was admitted to the thesis master's programme in Economics at Pamukkale University. He has held, and continues to hold, positions in various civil society organisations. He has also received expert-witness training in Qualified Calculations in Labour Law, Actuarial Qualified Calculations in Compensation Law and Qualified Calculations in Social Security Law. He is also a guitarist in the rock band S.S.Ç., plays two instruments and has an advanced command of English.

Verileriniz; talebinizin yerine getirilebilmesi için işlenmesinin gerekli olması ve işbu metinle vereceğiniz açık rıza hukuki sebeplerine dayanılarak işlenir.

Yasal saklama yükümlülüğü doğuran hâller saklıdır.

Kişisel verilerinizin işlenip işlenmediğini öğrenme; işlenmişse buna ilişkin bilgi talep etme; işlenme amacını ve amaca uygun kullanılıp kullanılmadığını öğrenme; eksik veya yanlış işlenmişse düzeltilmesini; KVKK’da öngörülen şartlarla silinmesini veya yok edilmesini isteme; işlemenin hukuka aykırılığı nedeniyle doğan zararın giderilmesini talep etme haklarına sahipsiniz. İşbu Aydınlatma Metni’ni okudum ve anladım. Yukarıda belirtilen kişisel verilerimin, açıklanan amaçlarla ve KVKK çerçevesinde işlenmesine ve saklanmasına özgür irademle açık rıza veriyorum.

1. Data Controller

The personal data you submit through the meeting/appointment form on this website is processed by Av. Bayram Can ÇAPAR - ÇAPAR HUKUK BÜROSU as data controller, under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”) and related legislation.

2. Personal Data Processed

Through the form, the following are processed: full name, e-mail address, telephone number, the subject/message you submit, and technical connection data (IP address and request date).

3. Purposes of Processing

Your data is processed solely to receive and evaluate your request, to contact you, to carry out the meeting or appointment process and, where applicable, to establish a legal advisory relationship. The data is not used for marketing or advertising.

Messages submitted through this form are completely deleted or irreversibly anonymised at the end of the day they are received (within 1 day at the latest). Statutory retention obligations are reserved.

1. Avukatlık Asgari Ücret Tarifesi (AAÜT)

Avukatlık hizmetlerinin ücreti, her yıl Türkiye Barolar Birliği tarafından belirlenip Resmî Gazete'de yayımlanan ve her yıl güncellenen Avukatlık Asgari Ücret Tarifesi (AAÜT) ile düzenlenir. Ücret, hizmetin niteliği ve süresine göre belirlenir ve AAÜT'de öngörülen asgari tutarın altında olamaz.

Bu bilgilendirmeyi okuyup onaylayarak; danışma/görüşme hizmetinin yürürlükteki AAÜT uyarınca ücrete tabi olduğunu bildiğinizi ve gerçekleşen danışma/görüşme için ilgili tarife uyarınca danışma ücretini ödemeyi kabul ettiğinizi beyan etmiş olursunuz. 1. Minimum Attorney Fee Tariff (AAÜT)

Attorney fees are governed by the Minimum Attorney Fee Tariff (AAÜT), set annually by the Union of Turkish Bar Associations, published in the Official Gazette and updated every year. Oral or written consultation and meeting services are subject to the consultation fee stipulated in the AAÜT for the year in which the service is provided.

2. This Form and the Fee

No legal opinion is provided through this form; your request is solely to arrange a meeting/appointment. When the meeting or consultation actually takes place, a fee accrues in accordance with the consultation tariff in the AAÜT in force on that date. The fee is determined by the nature and duration of the service and cannot be below the minimum amount stipulated in the AAÜT.

3. Consent
